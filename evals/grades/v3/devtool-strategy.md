## Errors

1. **Missed price contradiction, and the wrong price used as input.** The homepage shows Pro at "$20" (index.astro:403) and the pricing page at "$16" (pricing.astro:65). The answer opened pricing.astro but states "The Pro price is $20/mo" (A4) and feeds `arpaMonthly: 20` to all three `unit_economics` runs. At $16, every LTV and affordable-CAC figure would be 20% lower. Round 2 found this mismatch; round 3 drops it while listing five other site-vs-docs contradictions.
2. **"I could not check the separate analyzer repo" is false for this snapshot.** apps/analyzer is in the monorepo: Readme.md, action.yaml (a GitHub Action named "DBTool"), package.json with `"private": true` and no license field, and no LICENSE file. The tool log shows `ls` of apps/ was run. Rule 0: "Search the whole repo, not one folder". apps/analyzer/Readme.md also says the analyzer "analyzes your queries and suggests optimal indexes", which disagrees with ADR 0019 ("it never analyses"). That is one more docs-vs-docs contradiction the answer could have reported.
3. **Wrong line citations (3).** "getting-started.md L77" is L18. "getting-started.md L99" (twice) is L40, and the file is 94 lines long. The quoted text is right in all three.
4. **A playbook label misattributed.** "Bullseye ('one channel working well beats five') is [practitioner]." The quoted line is in channel-strategy "Sequencing rules" (knowledge/channel-strategy.md:48), which has no tag. The [practitioner] tag belongs to the Bullseye section, which does not contain that line.
5. **Data-handling check still incomplete.** Rule 0 says "check data-handling and credential claims … line by line, tooltips and FAQs included". Missed: (a) the pricing tooltip "Parameter values aren't included" (pricing.astro:263) against analyzer.mdx:21, "`pg_stat_statements` can contain real query text, including literal parameter values depending on your PostgreSQL configuration"; (b) the pricing FAQ "We're working on a fully self-hosted / offline mode" (pricing.astro:365) against the shipped self-hosting the answer itself lists; (c) the fact that the table statistics contain real column values (analyzer.mdx:28, introduction.md:59), the most sensitive data flow. The answer covers where data goes, not what is in it.
6. **ADRs cited without being opened.** "rewrites (introduction.md L37, ADRs 0031–0032)": the tool log lists only 0019 and 0023 as opened. The titles support the narrow claim, but rule 0 says "the file (and line) you actually opened".
7. **Overreaching inference.** "ADR 0019 says the Collector 'never analyses', so the open-source part produces no output to audit." A Collector that only sends still produces a payload that can be audited. The homepage's "audit the output" is weaker than the docs, but it is not empty.
8. **Bundled moves and missing tests** (rule 6: "each is one action, not a bundle … the cheapest test"). Move 2 bundles about five changes, and Move 3 bundles three launches. Moves 2 and 3 have no cheapest test or cost.
9. **Ordering contradiction.** "Fix these first; they are trust and legal problems" vs Move 2 "(weeks 3–6)", after interviews.
10. **Mixed bases in the Lifetime comparison (minor, carried over).** "$100 is below the modelled lifetime gross profit … ($156–$355)": $100 is revenue (about $80 gross profit at the assumed 80% margin). The conclusion holds. The answer also advises "cap the number and end date" without noting that the FAQ already says "Limited spots. When they're gone, we close it" (pricing.astro).
11. **Over the length limit.** 1,434 words against "under about 1,200".
12. **Unexplained jargon** (item 10): Show HN/HN, Bullseye, Dunford, cohort, switch interviews, stdio.
