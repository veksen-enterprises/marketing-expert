## Errors

1. **Factual (repo misread):** "Keep the 'add item on the website' path working well, so you have supply Discord doesn't control." In the app, `/create` (and `/planner`, the character dashboard) sit under `apps/app/src/routes/_admin-only.tsx` behind `AdminGate` and are marked `admin: true` in the nav. Today nobody but admins can add items on the website, so this mitigation is a build task, not something to "keep working". VISION describes it as an intended path, so the slip is understandable, but the answer presents it as live.
2. **Missed existing asset:** the API has a public new-item announce feed (`apps/api/src/services/discord-public-log.ts`, `announce-item.ts`) that posts every newly listed item to a public Discord channel. That is a ready-made demand-side surface: a channel traders can follow, a place for "watch this" prompts, a demo. The tool log shows a grep for "announce" in `apps/bot/src` only, so this was never found. Move 2 proposes building visibility loops without mentioning the one that exists.
3. **Misapplied citation:** "set up watches for 20–50 active traders in that server (the playbook says to seed 20–50 members by hand [practitioner])". The 20–50 rule in organic-social-and-community.md is about seeding members of a community you own before launch, a practice the same answer advises against ("Don't build your own Discord community"). Moving the number over to "watchers" gives a made-up threshold the look of support from a playbook.
4. **Plan feasibility not checked:** "set up watches for 20–50 active traders" on their behalf. A watcher needs an account to be notified (VISION open question 2, identity model). Setting watches for other people either needs them to sign in (then it isn't "by hand") or bypasses consent. Not wrong, but it skips the identity question the answer itself raises later.
5. **Omission with factual weight:** "I haven't checked what exists" for trade alternatives. No web access is a fair constraint, but the assistant knows d2jsp (the dominant PC GameX trade forum with its own forum-gold economy), TradeSite and tradesite.example and could have named them labelled "[own knowledge, unverified]", as it did for Reddit. They decide the positioning: GameX Companion's edge is "you don't have to list anywhere; posts you already make become searchable and fire watches fast", and that only stands out next to sites where you list by hand.
6. **No tool misuse.** The tool log is consistent with the answer, and it rightly skipped the economics step with a stated reason.

## Top strengths

1. **Spotted that moderators are the gatekeepers.** "your real 'first customers' are not traders. They are **the moderators of one or two active trade servers**", based on the bot README's channel-permission and 100-server intent details. This is the right non-obvious insight and it comes from the repo, not a template.
2. **Diagnosis that can be proven wrong, with per-market metrics.** Liquidity named as the constraint, an explicit falsifier, and metrics tied to the product ("Watch fire rate", "Post-to-DM time", "per server and never as a global total").
3. **Vision-aware "don'ts".** No owned community, no paid, no SEO push until item lifecycle is settled, `noindex` for stale item pages. Each is tied to the founder's principles or open questions.

## Server attribution (ranked, most impactful first)

1. **Make "competitive alternatives" an explicit, mandatory step in the strategy workflow.** *Server caused, assistant also partly ignored it.* INSTRUCTIONS #1 says to establish "the competitive alternatives", but the `marketing_strategy` prompt (src/prompts.ts, lines ~49–60) the assistant used as its outline has no alternatives step, and `marketing_diagnosis` step 1 only restates the business model. The outline won.
   Fix (src/prompts.ts, `marketing_strategy` and `marketing_diagnosis`): add a step after the business-type step: "Competitive alternatives: list what the target users do today without this product: named products, communities, manual workarounds, doing nothing. Use the repo's research/ docs first. If you can't browse, name the ones you know and tag them [own knowledge, unverified]; don't hand this back as a question. Say what the product does that those don't, and let that shape the first move." Also add one line to INSTRUCTIONS #1: "Asking the user is not a substitute for naming the alternatives you already know."
2. **Add launch timing tied to outside events to the playbooks and the strategy prompt.** *Server caused.* `launches-and-gtm.md` has nothing on timing a launch to a demand spike, and `consumer-apps.md` has no seasonality. The only "season" hit is a win-back line in `retention-and-expansion.md`. Game ladder resets, expansions, tax season and school terms are the cheapest reach a small product gets.
   Fix: add a "Launch into a spike" section to `knowledge/launches-and-gtm.md` (calendar events in the user's market; prepare before the spike, launch at it, measure cohorts by event; tag [practitioner]). Add to `marketing_strategy` step 6: "Check the market's calendar (seasons, releases, resets, regulation dates; repo docs may mention them) and place the plan's launch steps against it."
3. **Cover non-transacting "overlay" products and free/community tools in a playbook.** *Server caused (gap); assistant handled it well anyway.* The business-type list in `marketing_strategy` step 2 has no fit for a free tool layered on someone else's community. Every candidate assumes revenue, and `marketplaces.md` assumes the platform hosts the transaction (take rate, leakage, payment flow). The assistant had to strip half the playbook and derive the moderator insight by itself; a weaker run would not.
   Fix: in `knowledge/marketplaces.md` add a section "Aggregators and overlays (you don't host the transaction)": supply comes from venues you don't own; venue owners/moderators are the first customer; consent of the people whose posts become supply; the venue can cut you off (link platform-and-feature-risk); you measure match and contact, not GMV. In `src/prompts.ts` step 4, change "Economics: run unit_economics..." to "If there is no revenue model yet, say so, skip to the time budget, and state what would have to be true for money later", so free products have a defined path instead of a skipped step.
4. **Add a liquidity / match-rate calculator.** *Server caused (missing tool); explains rubric 5 = 1.* The key go/no-go number for any marketplace-like product ("is there enough supply in one market for requests to get filled?") has no tool. `funnel_analysis` needs observed stage counts and cannot project.
   Fix: add `liquidity_estimate` in `src/lib/economics.ts` (or a new `src/lib/liquidity.ts`) and register it in `src/server.ts`. Inputs: supply events per day, share of supply matching a typical request/watch (range), number of open requests, time window. Outputs: expected matches per request per week, probability of zero matches in the window (Poisson), and the supply rate needed to hit a target fire rate. Reference it from `marketplaces.md` "Liquidity metrics" and from `marketing_strategy` step 4, so stop conditions become numeric with labelled assumptions.
5. **Make the number of moves consistent and require a stop condition on every move.** *Server caused the sprawl; the assistant missed Move 2's stop condition by itself.* INSTRUCTIONS #5 says "one or two" moves, `marketing_strategy` step 5 says "shortlist 3" channels, and step 6 says "at most 2–3 things at once". Faced with mixed counts, the answer grew side sections (risks, conversations, table) that act as extra moves.
   Fix: in src/prompts.ts `marketing_strategy`, state "Output at most 3 ranked moves. Every move has: mechanism, cheapest test, cost, metric, stop result. Anything else (risks, research) goes in one short 'also' list of at most 3 lines." Align INSTRUCTIONS #5 with the same number.
6. **Tell the assistant to inventory what's live before recommending it.** *Assistant caused; the server can make it less likely.* It recommended a website-create path that is admin-gated and missed an existing public announce feed.
   Fix: add to INSTRUCTIONS #0: "When a repo is attached, list existing distribution surfaces (bots, public feeds, share links, public pages, sitemaps, emails) and check which features are gated, flagged or admin-only before naming them as levers. Grep the whole repo, not one app."
7. **Make evidence tags in the playbooks travel with each claim.** *Server partly caused.* `platform-and-feature-risk.md` notes only in its Sources footer that every case was "seen via search snippets; nothing read in full", and the dated case table has no inline tags. The answer then tagged the cases "[first-party / press]" with no caveat.
   Fix: add an inline tag such as `[press; snippet-only]` to each row of the case table, and in `knowledge/_conventions.md` require inline tags on every quantified claim so that `search_playbooks` excerpts carry them. Separately (assistant caused): add one line to INSTRUCTIONS #4: "Don't move a number from one context to another (e.g. a community-seeding count to a different action); if you need a threshold, call it your own assumption."
