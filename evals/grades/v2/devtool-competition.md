## Errors

1. **Misapplied citation and inflated evidence label (section 4).** "incumbents usually win those fights (… Chandy & Tellis, research)". In the competing-with-incumbents playbook this claim is Christensen's, tagged "[practitioner/theory]". Chandy & Tellis (2000) is cited there for incumbents introducing radical innovations, the opposite framing. This is the load-bearing sentence of the "Honest read".
2. **Dropbox causality still overstated.** "That is how Dropbox survived 'you're a feature' (platform playbook, first-party filings)." The filings give user and revenue figures. "How it survived" is the playbook's synthesis from snippets, and one case is presented as the mechanism.
3. **Wrong source for a quote.** "Under ADR 0019, analysis now runs on the platform and the Collector 'analyzes none of it'." The quoted words are from apps/docs/.../getting-started.md:6, not ADR 0019. The substance matches ADR 0019's title ("the Collector only sends").
4. **License fact slightly wrong.** "The root `package.json` license field is empty." The root package.json has **no** license field. The empty `"license": ""` is in packages/core/package.json and packages/mcp-server/package.json, and apps/api is `"UNLICENSED"`. There is no LICENSE file. The underlying point (check the license before claiming "open source") is right, and stronger than the answer makes it.
5. **"Tools that don't exist" lacks one fact.** The hook example's `analyze_query` / `recommend_indexes` and the pricing page's `explain_query` do exist, but in the **retired stdio server** (packages/mcp-server/src/server.spec.ts lists `analyze_query`, `explain_query`, `recommend_indexes`). The hosted `/mcp` does not register them. So the conclusion holds for the documented setup, but saying "retired stdio names" would explain the drift and show where the fix goes. `suggest_indexes` and `predict_at_scale` appear in neither server.
6. **Status table overstates one row.** "CI gate + PR comment … Shipped (CI guide, ADR 0019/0024)". ADR 0003 (the PR comment as a handoff to per-query triage) is "Accepted, not fully built". ADR 0037 (what CI warns on and what it blocks on) is "Accepted, being built". ADR 0013 (schema-change approval) is "not fully built". The gate runs, but its rules are mid-change, so "shipped, gating rules being reworked" would be accurate. Similarly, "Query rewrites: Shipped" ignores ADR 0030's "built except the connected view".
7. **Copy cost still not quantified.** The heading says "(in their own revenue terms)", but the content is qualitative: "Copying costs them almost nothing", "priced per server". There are no list prices, no comparison of per-host vs flat $16, and no "unquantified" label. The playbook's decision rule 1 asks for "which revenue or margin it would destroy".
8. **Omission: the PG18 statistics-import risk was dropped.** Round 1 rightly flagged that PG18's `pg_restore_relation_stats` / `pg_restore_attribute_stats` let stock Postgres take injected statistics, which weakens the fork's lead. v2 makes the fork its "strongest protection" ("your patched Postgres fork, ADR 0022") and never mentions this. It bears directly on the "agent runs EXPLAIN on a dev database" threat the answer itself names.
9. **Move 1's judge is the company's own tool.** Under "Compare each agent's index or 'this is fast' claim with `optimize_query`", the referee is DBTool's own cost *estimate* (VISION: "Cost is the planner's estimate rather than a measured time"). A buyer-credible benchmark needs measured runtimes on a scaled dataset alongside the estimate. Without them, a skeptic can say the benchmark is circular.
10. **Partly answered risk questions.** Prompt step 4 asks for "dependence share, access clauses, who owns the customer". The answer covers access ("Know which terms clause could cut each one off"), but not the playbook's first question, about what share of users would still pay if a free good-enough version shipped. It also leaves out who owns the customer, and the Anthropic access-cut precedents in the playbook (Windsurf, Jan 2026 credential block) that bear on a channel built through Claude Code.
11. **Tool use.** No misuse. The `competitive_strategy` step 0 points to `marketing_diagnosis`, which was not consulted. That is minor, because the stage check was done anyway. The competitor facts are broadly right as far as I know: Performance Insights is moving into CloudWatch Database Insights, Supabase's index_advisor is built on hypopg, and Supabase and Neon have MCP servers. All are flagged unverified.
