# What a good answer notices (graders only)

Graders score with `rubric.md`; these notes say what "specific to this business" (item 6) and "diagnosis" (item 3) look like for the fictional cases. A good answer need not hit every point, but missing the first one in each case is a diagnosis error.

## ecom-ads (Fernhill Tea)
- Break-even ROAS at 62% gross margin is about 1.6 (paid_media_math), so 1.4 is below break-even on first orders; with 22% repeat, judge on more than first-order value. Platform-reported ROAS is modelled, not incremental.
- Revenue stayed flat while spend rose: likely saturation or creative fatigue, plus seasonality (May and December gift peaks).
- Cheap wins outside ads: no welcome series, no abandoned-cart email, 4,100 subscribers mailed irregularly; "opens" are inflated by Apple Mail Privacy Protection.
- Free shipping threshold ($50) sits above the $38 average order: a lever to test.
- The page: generic hero copy ("Steeped in Tradition", "world-class"), meta description "Tea.", newsletter discount fits the founders' 15% cap.
- Respects the principles: no deep discounts, no hard-to-cancel subscription.

## local-weekdays (Northside Paws)
- The problem is weekday demand, not awareness: weekends are full. Levers: rebooking at checkout (18% now), weekday-only offers or pricing, recurring appointments, nail-trim walk-ins as a weekday feeder, vets' referrals on weekdays.
- No deposit and a 9% no-show rate cost slots.
- Reviews are stale (last 5 months ago): ask every client, not only happy ones (Google's rule).
- Respects the 7-dogs-a-day cap: growth means a better mix, not more dogs.

## app-downloads (Stillwater)
- Downloads are not the constraint: D30 is 6% overall but 28% for users who log 3 sits in week 1. Activation comes before acquisition.
- Product page converts 31%, search is 71% of downloads: keyword work on name and subtitle, custom product pages, ratings prompt after a success moment.
- Apple Ads Basic: $1.90 per install but 3 trials from $600, so about $200 per trial; don't scale it.
- The r/Meditation post was removed: community rules matter.
- Respects "no streaks, no guilt notifications, no account": no streak mechanics or growth via contact uploads.

## marketplace-city (Kitlend)
- City B is not liquid (22% of searches find a match, 4 rentals a month) after 7 months; a third city would split the same thin effort. The usual answer is: fix liquidity in A and B first (liquidity_math).
- 41% of listings never rented; owners with an early rental list more: focus supply quality and first rentals.
- Runway is about 9 months and the season is May–September: timing risk.
- Respects local pickup only.

## services-growth (Ledgerline)
- Capacity is 10 clients before hiring; 2 of 3 discovery calls close. The constraint is qualified leads in a narrow niche, not conversion.
- The food-cost spreadsheet already travels between owners: a free tool or template with a contact path.
- Referral sources (accountants, a distributor's rep) can be made systematic: partnerships.
- The website brings non-restaurant leads: niche positioning on the site.
- Respects "restaurants only" and the named-bookkeeper principle.
