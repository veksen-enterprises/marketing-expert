# Stillwater (fictional evaluation case)

iOS meditation timer with ambient soundscapes and a streak-free "sit log". Solo developer, launched 11 months ago. Not on Android.

- Price: free with 4 sounds; Stillwater Plus $3.99 a month or $24.99 a year (7-day free trial) unlocks 40 sounds, custom intervals and Apple Watch app.
- Principles (from the developer): no streaks or guilt notifications; no ads; no account required; data stays on the device.
- App Store: name "Stillwater", subtitle "Calm Meditation Timer", 4.8 stars from 312 ratings, in Health & Fitness.
- Marketing so far: one Reddit post in r/Meditation at launch (removed by moderators), a Product Hunt launch (#9 of the day), Apple Ads Basic test for 3 weeks.
