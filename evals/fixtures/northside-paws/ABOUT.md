# Northside Paws (fictional evaluation case)

Dog grooming salon in a mid-size Canadian city (one location, opened 3 years ago). Owner plus one employed groomer.

- Services: full groom $75–$140 by size and coat, bath and tidy $45–$70, nail trim $18 (walk-in).
- Booking: online booking page (third-party tool) and phone. No deposit. No-show rate about 9%.
- Principles (from the owner): no sedation; dogs are never left in cages more than 30 minutes; won't take more than 7 dogs a day.
- Marketing so far: Google Business Profile (41 reviews, 4.6 stars, last review 5 months ago), a Facebook page, flyers at two vet clinics.
