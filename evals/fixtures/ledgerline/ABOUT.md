# Ledgerline (fictional evaluation case)

Bookkeeping and payroll firm for independent restaurants. Three people (two bookkeepers and the founder, a former restaurant controller). Seven years old.

- Offer: monthly bookkeeping $650–$1,400 by volume; payroll add-on $150/month; year-end package with the client's tax accountant.
- Clients: 38 restaurants, all in one metro area; 90% came from referrals (accountants, other owners, one food distributor's rep).
- Capacity: the team can take about 10 more clients before hiring; the founder wants to grow to 70 clients in 3 years and hire.
- Principles (from the founder): restaurants only; no "set and forget" software resale; every client gets a named bookkeeper.
- Marketing so far: a five-page website, a LinkedIn profile, nothing paid. A food-cost spreadsheet the founder made gets passed around between owners.
