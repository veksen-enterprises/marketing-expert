# Marea Studio (fictional evaluation case)

One brand and web designer in Mexico City, freelancing full time since 2025 after four years at a local branding agency. Works in Spanish and English.

- Offer: brand identity for early-stage software startups (logo, type, colour, a one-page brand guide) at US$3,500, and a landing page design in Figma at US$2,000. Two to three weeks each.
- Clients so far: 5 projects in 14 months. 3 came through a friend who works at a San Francisco startup; 2 came from the agency's old Mexican clients, at about half the price.
- Wants: steady work from US and Canadian startups, 2 projects a month.
- Presence: a portfolio site with 8 agency projects (no prices, no "what I offer" page, contact form only); a Dribbble profile with 300 followers; a LinkedIn profile whose headline says "Designer"; an Upwork profile made 6 months ago with 2 proposals sent and no reply.
- Payments: US clients paid by bank transfer through a payment app; fees and the exchange rate took about 4% on each.
- Principles (from him): no spec work, no logo contests, no work for gambling or crypto.
- Ideas he has heard: cold email 500 US founders from a bought list, post daily on X, join a design marketplace.
