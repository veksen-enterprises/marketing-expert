# Olivo Nutrición (fictional evaluation case)

One registered dietitian-nutritionist (dietista-nutricionista, member of her regional professional college) in Valencia, Spain, working alone and online since early 2026. Before that, six years employed at a hospital outpatient clinic.

- Offer: video consultations for adults managing digestive problems (IBS, reflux) and for people starting a plant-based diet. First consultation €70 (60 min), follow-ups €45 (30 min), a 3-month plan with 5 follow-ups at €250. Clients can be anywhere in Spain; two live in other EU countries.
- Clients: 4 paying, all friends of friends or former colleagues' relatives. No client has found her through search or social media.
- Presence: an Instagram account (900 followers, mostly people she knows) whose bio reads "Valencia · plant-based · coffee"; it doesn't say she takes clients or how to book. A one-page site with a booking link, last updated in March. A LinkedIn profile that still lists the hospital job as current. WhatsApp is how every client so far got in touch.
- Time: about 15 hours a week for the practice; she keeps a part-time clinic job.
- Principles (from her): no weight-loss promises, no before-and-after photos, no supplements sold, no paid groups that require referring people.
- Ideas she has heard: run Instagram ads, start a TikTok, list on a doctor-booking platform, offer free first consultations.
