# Fernhill Tea (fictional evaluation case)

Online shop selling loose-leaf tea and teaware, on Shopify. Founded 14 months ago by two people; one works on it full time.

- Customers: mostly women 28–55 in the US who already drink loose-leaf tea; about 30% of orders are gifts (peaks in November–December and May).
- Products: 42 teas ($14–$32 per 100 g tin), 3 starter kits ($45–$65), teaware.
- Principles (from the founders): no discount codes above 15%; no subscription "traps" (cancelling must take one click); plastic-free packaging.
- What they tried: Meta ads since month 3 (the main channel); an Instagram account with 2,300 followers; a newsletter (4,100 subscribers, sent irregularly); one influencer gifting round (12 boxes, 2 posts, no tracked sales).
