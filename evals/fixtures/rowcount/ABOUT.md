# Rowcount (fictional evaluation case)

Row and stitch counter for knitters and crocheters: web app and iOS app, synced. Solo developer, launched 14 months ago.

- Price: free for one project; Rowcount Plus $4 a month or $36 a year unlocks unlimited projects, chart import from PDF patterns, and sync.
- Principles (from the developer): no ads; no selling or sharing user data; patterns people import stay private.
- Users: mostly hobby knitters; a few pattern designers use it to test-knit their own patterns.
- Marketing so far: a launch post in r/knitting (14 months ago, about 300 upvotes), an Instagram account with 900 followers, a listing on a free-tools page of a knitting blog.
- An earlier marketing-expert run (August 2026) set two moves with stop lines; they are saved in the business profile.
