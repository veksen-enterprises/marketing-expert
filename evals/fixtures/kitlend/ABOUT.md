# Kitlend (fictional evaluation case)

Peer-to-peer camping gear rental website in two cities in the US Pacific Northwest. Owners list tents, packs, stoves and kayaks; renters pick up in person. Two co-founders, launched 20 months ago, $180k from friends and family, about 9 months of runway.

- Take rate: 15% from the owner, 5% service fee from the renter. Damage protection included up to $1,000 per rental.
- Principles (from the founders): local pickup only (no shipping); owners set their own prices; no gear that can't be inspected in person.
- Marketing so far: Instagram (5,800 followers), flyers at trailheads and outdoor shops, a referral credit ($15 for both sides), a partnership with one university outdoor club.
