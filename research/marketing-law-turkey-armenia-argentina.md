### What this means for a small product (Turkey)

- **Email and SMS:** opt-in, and the opt-in only counts once it sits in İYS. A founder who collects newsletter emails on their own site has three business days to upload each consent to İYS (Regulation Art. 7(11)–(12)). Messages to merchants and tradespeople (a café owner, a B2B buyer at a company) need no prior consent (Law 6563 Art. 6(2)), but refusals must be checked in İYS. Fines per sending are small in lira (TRY 2,859–14,309 in 2026, up to ten times for bulk), but complaints are cheap to file.
- **Cookies and analytics:** explicit consent before analytics and ad cookies; legitimate interest does not cover them (Decision 2022/229). This hits the free game companion site and the craft tool if they run Google Analytics or ad pixels.
- **Fitness game with health data:** health data is special-category (Art. 6); if processing it is the core business, the VERBIS exemption shrinks to under 10 employees and under TRY 10 million (Decision 2025/1572). A foreign controller registers in VERBIS through a Turkish representative regardless of size [vendor source].
- **Transfers abroad:** sending Turkish users' data to a US/EU cloud needs an adequacy decision (none known to be issued as of this research) or a Board standard contract, notified within five business days of signing (Art. 9(5)). Not checked: whether the Board has issued any adequacy decision.
- **Prices and discounts:** show the all-in TL price including taxes; strike-through prices must be the lowest of the previous 10 days (goods) from 1 August 2026.
- **Influencers:** "Reklam" or "Tanıtım" plus the brand name, visible without scrolling, from 1 August 2026.
- **Subscriptions:** cancellation can't be harder than sign-up; fixed-term plans can't auto-renew for another full term without consent; 14-day withdrawal, but instantly delivered digital content and services started with consent are excluded.
- **Reviews:** only verified buyers; no importing reviews from unverifiable platforms.

### What this means for a small product (Armenia)

- **Email and SMS:** no statute written for e-marketing, but the data protection law needs prior consent, the PDPA's advisory decision asks for opt-in and opt-out, and the competition authority fined bookmakers about AMD 1 billion in 2025 for advertising messages sent without consent. Treat marketing messages as opt-in.
- **Ads in Armenian:** any advertising aimed at Armenia must be in Armenian; English can sit beside it but not bigger, not first, and not saying more. This matters for app-store screenshots, social ads and billboards for the café app. Whether it reaches a foreign company's English-language website is not settled in what was read.
- **Health data:** the fitness game must notify the PDPA before processing special-category or biometric data.
- **Transfers:** consent covers most transfers; non-adequate countries otherwise need permission.
- **Consumer contracts:** 14-day withdrawal and a labelled pay button from 1 July 2026 (not read in the law itself). Prices in drams.
- **Enforcement:** the Competition Protection Commission is the active enforcer and fines are large (record AMD 3 billion in 2024), mainly for misleading claims like delivery times.

### What this means for a small product (Argentina)

- **Email:** no opt-in statute for marketing email; the general rule is consent for processing (Ley 25.326 Art. 5) and an always-available removal right for advertising data (Art. 27). Put an unsubscribe in every message and honour it.
- **Calls and phone-based messages:** check the No Llame register every 30 days before any phone marketing (Ley 26.951 Art. 7). AAIP fines are capped low per breach (ARS 100,000) but can be multiplied by up to 500 for repeated breaches.
- **Database registration:** the AAIP still requires databases to be registered (Art. 21); free, online via TAD with a tax login.
- **Withdrawal and cancel buttons:** any site or app selling at a distance needs a visible "Botón de arrepentimiento" on first access, and services (subscriptions) need a "Botón de baja de servicio". Courts confirmed ARS 2–4 million fines when the link was plain text or buried. This hits the craft tool's paid plan and the fitness game's subscription if sold through their own web checkout. Whether the buttons apply inside app-store billing was not checked.
- **Prices:** pesos always, dollars optional; show the final total. In a high-inflation economy, prices change often, so the advertised price must still be what the checkout charges (Art. 7 of Ley 24.240: offers bind for their duration).
- **Giveaways:** a draw can't require a purchase (DNU 274/2019 Art. 14(b)).
- **Spanish:** contracts and sale documents in Spanish (Ley 24.240 Art. 10); product labels in Spanish (DNU 274/2019 Art. 20).
- **Health data:** sensitive; nobody can be forced to provide it; get express consent and register the database.

## Folklore

- "Turkey's KVKK is a copy of GDPR, so legitimate interest covers analytics." The Board said non-essential cookies need explicit consent (Decision 2022/229).
- "B2B email is always free in Turkey." No prior consent is needed for merchants, but refusals must still be honoured and checked in İYS (Regulation Art. 6(3), 6(6)).
- "Turkish strike-through prices use the 30-day lowest price, like the EU." From 1 August 2026, goods use the 10-day lowest price (Art. 14(3) as amended).
- "Armenia has no spam law, so SMS blasts are fine." The competition authority fined bookmakers about AMD 1 billion in 2025 for advertising messages sent without consent.
- "Argentina's botón de arrepentimiento is Resolución 424/2020." It was repealed in September 2025 and replaced by Disposición 954/2025.
- "Argentina banned showing prices in dollars." Since January 2025 dollar prices may be shown in addition to pesos (Res. 4/2025).
