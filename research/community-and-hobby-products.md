## Sources

### Discord

1. **Discord, "Premium Apps Payout"** (Developer Help Center). https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/17299902720919-Premium-Apps-Payout — Developers receive 85% of payments (less payment processing and transaction fees) for the first US$1 million in cumulative gross sales per developer team; after that the platform fee returns to the standard 30%. Payout review after the first US$100 earned; payouts within 45 days after month end (later payouts only above US$25). [first-party, read 2026-10-05]
2. **Discord blog, "Premium App Subscriptions for Discord developers"** and **developer change log / December 2025 developer newsletter**. https://discord.com/blog/premium-app-subscriptions-for-discord-developers ; https://docs.discord.com/developers/change-log ; https://discord.com/developer-newsletter/december-2025 — Subscriptions can be per-server (whole community uses it) or per-user (works across servers). Later additions: one-time purchases (durable and consumable items), a Store page reachable from the App Directory and the bot profile, multiple subscription SKUs, purchases inside Activities, analytics export (CSV, April 2024). [first-party, read 2026-10-05]
3. **Discord, "Premium Apps' Required Support for Monetizing Apps".** https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/23810643331735 — Teams that monetize on Discord must offer supported offerings through Premium Apps, and the price there must not be higher than through other payment options (effective 7 Oct 2024; applies only where Premium Apps supports the developer's locale and offering type). [first-party, read 2026-10-05]
4. Eligibility for Premium Apps: app must be verified, owned by a Developer Team, and the team based in a supported locale; the Payout article (updated June 2026) still names US, UK and EU. [first-party, read 2026-10-05]
5. **Discord, "Changes to Privileged Intent Access for Discord Apps".** https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/40281523410967 — Old rule: apps in 100+ servers had to apply for privileged intents (members, presences, message content). New rule: apps below 10,000 users (unique users who can see the app across its servers) can toggle them on; at 10,000 users they must apply. Apps already approved must reapply once a year, with a 90-day window after notice; notices go out through 31 Dec 2026 (article dated May 2026: "between now and the end of this year"). App Verification and privileged-intent review are now separate processes, and apps are no longer blocked from joining servers while under review. [first-party, read 2026-10-05]
6. **Discord, "Getting Started with Privileged Intent Review" / "Message Content Intent Review Policy".** https://docs.discord.com/developers/gateway/getting-started-with-privileged-intent-review — Review asks why the app needs the data, how it is stored or discarded, and what user-facing value it gives. App verification at 100 servers is described in many community sources [not re-verified on primary page this session].
7. **Discord Developer Policy.** https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/8563934450327-Discord-Developer-Policy — Use API data only as needed for the app's stated functionality; improvement uses only on aggregated or de-identified data; no selling, licensing or commercialising API data; no disclosure to data brokers or ad networks; no training ML/AI models on message content without Discord's express permission; the duty to delete API data when Discord or the user asks, when the data is no longer needed, or when the app stops operating is in the Discord Developer Terms of Service §5, not the Developer Policy (https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/8562894815383). [first-party, read 2026-10-05; corrected]
8. **Discord Community Guidelines / Platform Manipulation Policy Explainer.** https://discord.com/guidelines — "Do not use self-bots or user-bots. Each account must be associated with a human, not a bot." [first-party, read 2026-10-05]

### Evidence on side projects and maintainers

19. **Folta, Delmar & Wennberg, "Hybrid Entrepreneurship," *Management Science* 56(2):253–269, 2010.** https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1477325 — Swedish register data. People who start a business while keeping a paid job ("hybrid entrepreneurs") are a significant share of all entrepreneurs, and hybrid entry may make later entry into full self-employment easier (eight years of matched employee-employer data, Swedish knowledge-intensive sector). The abstract makes no survival claim. [research, read 2026-10-05 (abstract via Crossref); corrected] The survival finding belongs to Raffiee & Feng, "Should I Quit My Day Job?: A Hybrid Path to Entrepreneurship", *Academy of Management Journal* 57(4), 2014 (https://journals.aom.org/doi/abs/10.5465/amj.2012.0522): risk-averse people and people with low core self-evaluation are more likely to enter hybrid rather than full-time; hybrid entrepreneurs who later quit their day job have "much higher rates of survival" than people who go full time directly from paid employment, and the effect is stronger with prior entrepreneurial experience. [research, read 2026-10-05 (abstract via OpenAlex)]
20. **Tidelift State of the Open Source Maintainer reports (2021, 2023, 2024).** https://www.theregister.com/2024/09/18/open_source_maintainers_underpaid/ — About 60% of surveyed maintainers are not paid for maintenance; about a quarter report income from donation programs such as GitHub Sponsors (16% in 2021, 24% in 2023, 25% in 2024); about 5% report direct payments from individuals. [vendor: Tidelift sells paid maintenance; self-selected survey]
