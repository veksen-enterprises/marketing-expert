## Sources

### Discord

1. **Discord, "Premium Apps Payout"** (Developer Help Center). https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/17299902720919-Premium-Apps-Payout — Developers receive 85% of payments (less payment processing and transaction fees) for the first US$1 million in cumulative gross sales per developer team; after that the platform fee returns to the standard 30%. Payout review after the first US$100 earned; payouts within 45 days after month end. [first-party, snippet-only]
2. **Discord blog, "Premium App Subscriptions for Discord developers"** and **developer change log / December 2025 developer newsletter**. https://discord.com/blog/premium-app-subscriptions-for-discord-developers ; https://docs.discord.com/developers/change-log ; https://discord.com/developer-newsletter/december-2025 — Subscriptions can be per-server (whole community uses it) or per-user (works across servers). Later additions: one-time purchases (durable and consumable items), a Store page reachable from the App Directory and the bot profile, multiple subscription SKUs, purchases inside Activities, analytics export. [first-party, snippet-only]
3. **Discord, "Premium Apps' Required Support for Monetizing Apps".** https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/23810643331735 — Teams that monetize on Discord must offer supported offerings through Premium Apps, and the price there must not be higher than through other payment options. [first-party, snippet-only]
4. Eligibility for Premium Apps: app must be verified, owned by a Developer Team, and the team based in a supported locale; snippets named US, UK and EU. [first-party via snippet; locale list not re-verified]
5. **Discord, "Changes to Privileged Intent Access for Discord Apps".** https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/40281523410967 — Old rule: apps in 100+ servers had to apply for privileged intents (members, presences, message content). New rule: apps below 10,000 users (unique users who can see the app across its servers) can toggle them on; at 10,000 users they must apply. Apps already approved must reapply once a year, with a 90-day window after notice; notices go out through 31 Dec 2026. [first-party, snippet-only]
6. **Discord, "Getting Started with Privileged Intent Review" / "Message Content Intent Review Policy".** https://docs.discord.com/developers/gateway/getting-started-with-privileged-intent-review — Review asks why the app needs the data, how it is stored or discarded, and what user-facing value it gives. App verification at 100 servers is described in many community sources [not re-verified on primary page this session].
7. **Discord Developer Policy.** https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/8563934450327-Discord-Developer-Policy — Use API data only as needed for the app's stated functionality; improvement uses only on aggregated or de-identified data; no selling, licensing or commercialising API data; no disclosure to data brokers or ad networks; no training ML/AI models on message content without Discord's express permission; delete end-user data on Discord's or the user's request (snippet also mentions a deadline after account termination; exact wording not re-verified). [first-party, snippet-only]
8. **Discord Community Guidelines / Platform Manipulation Policy Explainer.** https://discord.com/guidelines — "Do not use self-bots or user-bots. Each account must be associated with a human, not a bot." [first-party, snippet-only]

### Money tools

16. **Patreon fee change** (Patreon "Founders plan" help page; Lindsey Gamble blog; others). https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/9943809610253 ; https://www.lindseygamble.com/blog/patreon-increases-its-prices-for-new-creators — Pages launched from 4 Aug 2025 go on a single Standard plan at 10% platform fee, plus payment processing (commonly quoted 2.9% + $0.30; higher percentage on small pledges). [first-party, snippet-only]

Gold price and whether new accounts pay 5% on tips differ between sources. [not re-verified]

### Evidence on side projects and maintainers

19. **Folta, Delmar & Wennberg, "Hybrid Entrepreneurship," *Management Science* 56(2):253–269, 2010.** https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1477325 — Swedish register data. People who start a business while keeping a paid job ("hybrid entrepreneurs") are a significant share of all entrepreneurs; those who later go full time have much higher survival than people who enter full-time self-employment directly. People with more uncertainty about their ability and higher opportunity costs prefer hybrid entry. [research, snippet-only] Related: Raffiee & Feng, "Should I Quit My Day Job?", *Academy of Management Journal*, 2014 (https://journals.aom.org/doi/abs/10.5465/amj.2012.0522) — title found; finding commonly summarised as hybrid entrants having lower failure risk [not re-verified].
20. **Tidelift State of the Open Source Maintainer reports (2021, 2023, 2024).** https://www.theregister.com/2024/09/18/open_source_maintainers_underpaid/ — About 60% of surveyed maintainers are not paid for maintenance; about a quarter report income from donation programs such as GitHub Sponsors (16% in 2021, 24% in 2023, 25% in 2024); about 5% report direct payments from individuals. [vendor: Tidelift sells paid maintenance; self-selected survey]
