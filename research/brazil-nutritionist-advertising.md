## 1. Which code is in force

- Res. 856/2026 revokes Res. 599/2018 (Art. 116) and takes effect "após decorridos 90 (noventa) dias de sua publicação" (Art. 117). Published 28 April 2026, so about 27 July 2026 by count. [law, 1, read; fetch summary; Brazil; date derived]
- After protests the CFN opened a consultation (13 May–13 June 2026) and said the deadline "poderá ser prorrogado" and that articles may still be adjusted. [first-party, 5; May 2026] In June the president said the code stays, with some articles changed. [press, 7; June 2026] No amendment or extension was found. Whether it took effect unchanged in July is not confirmed. [derived; gap]

## 2. Advertising under the new code (2026)

- **Results, before-and-after:** Art. 69 §2: "não é permitida, mesmo com autorização concedida, a apresentação dos resultados de si ou de terceiros, tais como imagens, composição corporal, dados laboratoriais, exames e gráficos, inclusive aqueles gerados por IA." No showing results of yourself or clients (photos, body composition, lab data, charts, AI images), even with consent. Only technical-scientific settings, with identity hidden and no advertising slant, are exempt (§3). [law, 1, read; fetch summary; Brazil; 2026]
- **Testimonials:** no article uses "depoimento" or "testemunho". [law, 1; text search via fetch tool; may miss] The CFN president: "O nutricionista pode divulgar o depoimento do paciente." [press, 7; 16 June 2026] Under the 2018 code, a CRN-3 ethics committee said even a client's thank-you post comparing before and after could be punished. [first-party, 9; 2018; one regional view]
- **Prices:** Art. 68 makes publishing prices and fees a right, without misleading terms, with name and CRN number (Art. 24). [law, 1; 2026] But Art. 68 III: "É vedado … realizar ofertas, promoções, sorteios de procedimentos, produtos ou serviços como forma de publicidade". No discounts-as-ads, no giveaways. [law, 1; 2026] The 2018 code banned using fees too (Art. 57). [law, 2; 2018–2026]
- **Promises:** Art. 69 §6 bans claiming "a garantia dos resultados". [law, 1; 2026] The 2018 code also required saying results "podem não ocorrer da mesma forma para todos" (Art. 55). [law, 2]
- **Brands:** Art. 74 bars linking one's image to food, supplement, equipment or lab brands, "seja presencialmente, por Telenutrição, seja em mídias sociais", with listed exceptions (owner, technical lead) and a declared conflict of interest. [law, 1; 2026; exceptions per a secondary summary]
- **AI:** Art. 38 bans generative AI that simulates real people or clinical results. [law, 1; 2026]

## 3. Telenutrition

- Res. 760/2023 governs it, in force since 24 October 2023; it revoked Res. 666/2020. [law, 3, read; fetch summary; Brazil] The 2026 manual still cites it. [first-party, 4; Sept 2026]
- Conditions: active CRN registration (Art. 2 I); prior registration in e-Nutricionista (Art. 5); a signed consent form, TCLE, before the first session (Art. 9 VI); no recording of sessions with sensitive data (Art. 9 VII); records in a prontuário (Art. 10–11). No in-person first visit is required; in-person "permanece como padrão de referência" (Art. 6). [law, 3; 2023]
- Art. 12 §1 still bans using fees, promotions and giveaways to advertise telenutrition. This conflicts with the 2026 code's right to publish prices. [law, 3; derived on conflict]
- **Other states:** acts "terão validade no território nacional"; no secondary registration for practice in another region done only by telenutrition (Art. 4, 13; matches Lei 14.510/2022 as cited by the manual). [law, 3–4; 2023–2026]
- **Abroad:** Art. 4 §2: "o nutricionista deverá atender à regulamentação e legislação do país em questão." The manual adds that it "pode, ainda, ser caracterizado como exercício ilegal de profissão" there. [law, 3; first-party, 4]

## 4. The client's side

- **Portugal:** the Ordem holds "em exclusivo" the title nutricionista and denounces "exercício ilegal da profissão". [law, 11; fetch summary; excerpt] No rule on remote care from abroad was seen. [gap]
- **US:** the client's state at time of service sets the rule (`regulated-health-pro-advertising.md` §4). Of 52 jurisdictions, 36 license dietitians; 3 protect titles only; 4 have no statute. [first-party, 12; undated] [corrected 2026-10-28: the chart gives no totals; counting its rows by first-listed type gives 38 licensure (including Nebraska, "medical nutrition therapist", and Montana, nutritionists only), 7 certification, 3 title protection only, 4 no statute, of 52] Some licensing states allow "general non-medical nutrition information" while still licensing medical nutrition therapy (Ohio from 2025). [interested body, 13; one state; how many states do this not counted]
- **Canada:** registration in the client's province in most provinces (`regulated-health-pro-advertising.md` §4).
- So the safe offer abroad is general nutrition education (classes, content, group programmes), not individual assessment or a diet prescription, and not the reserved title where it is protected. [derived from 3, 11–13; not legal advice]

## 5. Consumer and privacy law (cited, not read)

- CDC Art. 31 (price information) is cited by the code itself (Art. 68 II). [law, 1] CDC Art. 37 bans misleading advertising; LGPD treats health data as sensitive and needs a legal basis such as consent for marketing messages; CONAR's code covers health claims. [not read; planalto.gov.br refused]

## Where this agrees and differs from Quebec

- Agrees: true testimonials allowed (Quebec s.76(3)); no misleading claims; follow the rule where the client is.
- Differs: Brazil bans showing any results, even with consent; Quebec has no such clause (Ontario's photo rule only needs typical results). Brazil bans promotions and giveaways. Within Brazil no extra registration; across Canadian provinces it is needed.
