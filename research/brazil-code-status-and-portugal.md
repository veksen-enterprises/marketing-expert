# Is Brazil's new nutritionist code (CFN Res. 856/2026) in force, and does Portugal require registration for a nutritionist serving its residents remotely from outside the EU? (checked 2026-10-28)

Question: `brazil-nutritionist-advertising.md` proposes a Brazil section for `knowledge/marketing-law-by-market.md` built on CFN Res. 856/2026, which by its own count took effect about 27 July 2026, with a caveat that the CFN might move the date. Did it take effect, was it amended, and was Res. 760/2023 Art. 12 §1 (no fees in telenutrition ads) changed to match? Separately: must a nutritionist established in Brazil register with Portugal's Ordem dos Nutricionistas before serving a client in Portugal online?

## 1. Res. 856/2026 is not yet in force: postponed to 23 January 2027

- CFN Res. 858/2026, dated 20 July 2026 and published in the DOU on 24 July 2026, Art. 1: "Prorrogar o prazo de vacância da Resolução CFN nº 856, de 25 de abril de 2026, por 180 (cento e oitenta) dias, a contar de 27 de julho de 2026, passando a viger a partir do dia 23 de janeiro de 2027." The start date of the new code moves 180 days, to 23 January 2027. [law, 1, read; fetch summary; Brazil; in force since 24 Jul 2026]
- Art. 2 revokes Res. 856's own start-date article (Art. 117). Art. 3: in force on publication. [law, 1; 2026] The count checks: 27 July plus 180 days is 23 January. [derived]
- Res. 858 changes no other article of Res. 856. [law, 1; 2026] LegisMap's copy of Res. 856 does not show Res. 858, so it lags. [law, 2; secondary host]
- The CFN had said in May that "a prorrogação do prazo era possível a qualquer tempo e por qualquer período" (an extension was possible at any time, for any length) and opened a 30-day consultation. [first-party, 5; 8 May 2026] Res. 858 is that extension. Whether the consultation leads to amended articles before January is not known. [derived; gap]
- So Res. 599/2018 stays in force until 23 January 2027. Res. 856's revocation of it (Art. 116) is part of a resolution not yet in force. [derived from 1–2; no CFN statement seen saying so]

## 2. Which advertising rules apply now

- Until 23 January 2027, the 2018 code's rules apply, as the hop-0 note gives them: no use of fees as advertising (Art. 57), results "podem não ocorrer da mesma forma para todos" (results may differ between people; Art. 55), and the CFN's 2018 reading that before-and-after photos are banned. [law; hop-0 note sources 2 and 9; 2018–Jan 2027]
- From 23 January 2027, Res. 856 applies as published unless changed first: right to publish prices (Art. 68), no offers, promotions or giveaways as ads (Art. 68 III), no showing results even with consent (Art. 69 §2). [law, 2; quoted in hop-0 note; from 23 Jan 2027]

## 3. Res. 760/2023 Art. 12 §1 is unchanged

- Art. 12 §1 still reads: "sendo vedado ao nutricionista utilizar o valor de seus honorários, promoções e sorteios de procedimentos ou serviços como forma de publicidade e propaganda da Telenutrição" (a nutritionist may not use fees, promotions or giveaways to advertise telenutrition). No amendment is annotated. [law, 3, read; fetch summary; secondary host; Brazil; in force since 24 Oct 2023]
- Res. 856 does not mention Res. 760. [law, 2; text search by fetch tool] Res. 858 does not either. [law, 1]
- The September 2026 manual still cites Res. 760 as current and Res. 599 as the code. It does not mention Res. 856 or 858. Its checklist says "Informe preços e formas de pagamento" (tell clients prices and payment terms), which fits Art. 12 §1: fees are banned in ads, not in talks with a client. [first-party, 4; Sept 2026; reading derived]
- Until January 2027 the two rules agree: fees may not be used in ads (Res. 599 Art. 57; Res. 760 Art. 12 §1). From January the conflict the hop-0 note found will exist unless the CFN fixes it. [derived]

## 4. Portugal: a non-EU nutritionist has no light route

- Practice needs full membership: practising as a nutricionista "dependem da inscrição como membro efetivo na Ordem dos Nutricionistas" (depends on registration as a full member). [law, 7, read; fetch summary; Portugal; in force since Dec 2021]
- Occasional service by someone established elsewhere ("em regime de livre prestação de serviços") is set by Estatuto Art. 73. But the regulation gives that route only to EU/EEA nationals. [law, 7; 2021; Art. 73 itself not read] Such providers are held to the Ordem's discipline and can be banned from practising in Portugal. [law, 8; 2016; since replaced by Reg. 1333/2024, whose matching articles were not seen]
- A holder of a non-Portuguese, non-EU degree can register only after the degree is recognised as equivalent, and then as a trainee (membro estagiário). [law, 7; 2021]
- The Ordem holds the title nutricionista "em exclusivo" and reports "exercício ilegal da profissão". [law, 10; 2015; agrees with hop-0 note]
- The Estatuto was amended by Lei 78/2023. [law, 9; named in a 2024 preamble; the amending text was not read]
- Not found: any article saying whether serving a Portugal resident by video, from Brazil, counts as practice in Portugal. Nothing read covers remote care. So "Portugal requires registration for remote service" is not confirmed. It is also not ruled out: a Brazilian provider has no free-provision route to fall back on. [derived; gap]

## Where this agrees and disagrees with the hop-0 note

- Agrees: Res. 856's text; Res. 760 governs telenutrition; Art. 12 §1 is unrevoked; Portugal reserves the title.
- Disagrees: its "about 27 July 2026" start date is out of date. Res. 856 starts 23 January 2027. Its §2 is titled "Advertising under the new code (2026)", but none of it binds anyone yet. The conflict in its §3 starts only in 2027.

## What this changes

1. The proposed Brazil section in `knowledge/marketing-law-by-market.md` should say: "Code in force: Res. 599/2018 until 22 January 2027; Res. 856/2026 from 23 January 2027 (postponed by Res. 858/2026, DOU 24 July 2026) [law; CFN Res. 858/2026 Art. 1; checked 2026-10-28]." It should list today's bans (no fees as ads, results caveat, no before-and-after) and the 2027 changes separately, and keep a caveat that the CFN may amend articles before then. Test: `search_playbooks` for "Brazil nutritionist prices" returns a chunk containing "23 January 2027"; the readme and consistency tests still pass.
2. The catalog note proposed in the hop-0 note (point 3) should not say "no showing results, even with consent" as current law until January 2027. Under Res. 599 the ban covers before-and-after photos; say that, with the date.
3. The "clients abroad" line stays as proposed. Portugal is not named, since remote registration was not confirmed. Its "unless registered there" already covers Portugal.
