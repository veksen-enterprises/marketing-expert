## 1. Product Hunt: two months and a significant update

- "We ask Makers to wait at least two months between posts for the same product or from the same company." There "also needs to be a significant update to the product." New interfaces and pricing tweaks don't qualify. Under two months needs a relaunch request, reviewed by the team. [first-party, 1]
- Resubmitting within two months triggers a prompt asking whether this is a major update. The stated reason for the rule: the home page got "too homogeneous from multiple quick-fire launches from the same makers." [first-party, 3]
- The launch guide encourages repeat launches: "You can launch again every time you ship a major feature or update." It cites Loom as having "launched 15 times". [first-party, 2; one selected success]
- The widely repeated "six months between launches" is not in any Product Hunt page read here. The blog that states it calls it "unwritten". [practitioner, 11]. Treat it as folklore.
- Product Hunt records an update under the original product page, and it goes through the same ranking as a first launch. [research, 13, read; preprint; data appendix]

## 2. Hacker News: a new Show HN needs a significantly different version

- Show HN: "New features and upgrades ('Foo 1.3.1 is out') generally aren't substantive enough to be Show HNs. A major overhaul is probably ok." [first-party, 4]
- A moderator: "You can post a new release as a Show HN only if the new version is significantly different." Link the previous Show HN and say what changed. "This should probably only happen once or twice a year." [first-party, 6; moderator comment]
- General reposts: "If a story has not had significant attention in the last year or so, a small number of reposts is ok. Otherwise we bury reposts as duplicates." Don't delete and repost. [first-party, 5]
- So the rule turns on the first post's result. A post that got no attention may be posted again a small number of times. A post that got attention can't be, unless the product changed a lot. [derived from 4–7]
- The second-chance pool is picked by moderators and reviewers, not requested by the poster. Anyone may email a suggestion, including their own post; some posters are invited by email to repost. [first-party, 8; c. 2021]

## 3. BetaList and Reddit

- BetaList: "We generally only feature each startup once," except a startup featured pre-launch may be featured again at launch. A rejected startup may resubmit after improving. [first-party, 9]
- Reddit has no sitewide repost or self-promotion ratio; each community sets its own (app-discovery-outside-stores.md item 36). No subreddit's rule was read this session. [first-party, via that note]

## 4. Outcomes of second launches: no data with a denominator

- No source compared a product's second launch with its first on any site. PHBench says companies "can appear multiple times" and groups them to avoid leakage, but gives no count or outcome for re-launches. [research, 12, read; preprint]
- One founder's Product Hunt launches got 20, then 8 upvotes; the gap and what changed between them are not stated. [practitioner, 10; one case]
- The only decay data is for identical images on Reddit, already in proven-channel-returns.md §3 (source 5 there). It does not cover a new version.
- Any repost data from HN would be selected on failure: the rules allow a plain repost only when the first got no attention. A better second result would partly be regression to the mean. [derived from 5]

## 5. Product Hunt featured share by year (rough)

- PHBench's featured posts in its train and validation splits: 2022: 8,448; 2023: 11,991; 2024: 5,826. A 2025 cohort of 4,076 posts is excluded from the year table. [research, 12, read; preprint]
- Those splits hold about 80% of the 67,292 featured posts (53,824). If the test split is not tied to year, 2024 had about 7,300 featured posts and 2023 about 15,000. [derived from 12; splits are by company, so year balance is assumed, not checked]
- Launches: 41,169 in 2023 [research, 13, read; counts "ventures", a different unit from PHBench's posts]; 2,900–4,900 a month from about February 2024 (design-and-marketing-in-crowded-markets.md item 4, practitioner).
- Rough shares: 2023 about 36%; 2024 about 12–21%. The featured count roughly halved while launches rose. [derived; mixed units and a practitioner denominator]
- This does not confirm "about 10%" for a whole year. The 10% figure is for one month (September 2024) and its original analysis was not found (same note, item 5).

## What this changes

1. **The reason text in proven-channel-returns.md item 2: state the real rule.** Agree with dropping the boost. Replace the reason with: "You already launched on one site. Launching there again needs real news: Product Hunt asks for two months and a significant update; Show HN asks for a much changed version. Try a site you haven't used." Test (in `test/smallBets.test.ts`): a launched profile with `channels: [{ name: "Product Hunt", how: "referrer" }]` → `launch-sites` in fitsNow, unboosted, reason matches `/two months/` and `/site you haven't used/`.
2. **`launch-sites` stop line: agree with small-bet-base-rates.md item 1 (`tries: 2`, `effortHours` about 24, a no-reaction branch), with one change: the two tries are on two different sites.** Its draft says "no reaction on two sites", which already implies this. Add one sentence so the second try is not read as a repost: "Don't post the same version to the same site again." Proposed `stop`: "No reaction on two different sites (almost no votes or comments): rewrite the first line and the demo before a third. Don't post the same version to the same site again. A spike with no retained users: the product, not the channel, needs work." Test: `stop` matches `/two different sites/` and `/same version to the same site/`.
3. **No change from the featured share.** The rough 2024 share (12–21%) is low but the stop line already judges on reaction, not featuring. `evidence` stays "anecdote": nothing here counts users.

## What this does not settle

- Whether a second launch does better or worse than a first. No denominator anywhere.
- Subreddit and Indie Hackers rules: not read.
- What Product Hunt's team accepts as "significant": only the two exclusions (interfaces, pricing) are stated.
- The featured shares rest on an assumed year balance across PHBench splits and on a practitioner launch count; both could move the 2024 figure a lot.
- The HN moderator comments are years old and on a mirror; the 2026 talk of limiting Show HN posts from new accounts was seen only in a secondary blog with no link to a rule.
