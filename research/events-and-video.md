## Sources

### Events and webinars

1. **ON24. *Webinar Benchmarks Report* (2025 edition, covering 2024 webinars) and ON24 blog "Key Takeaways from the 2025 Webinar Benchmarks Report".** https://www.on24.com/blog/key-takeaways-from-the-2025-webinar-benchmarks-report/ ; https://www.on24.com/blog/key-takeaways-from-the-webinar-benchmarks-report/
   - Snippets give registrant-to-attendee conversion of about 57% (2025 report, 2024 data) and 60% (described as the latest/2026 data); on-demand viewers about 43%–52% of attendees depending on page and year ("on-demand viewing accounts for 50% of all webinar attendees"); slow rise in on-demand share of about 1 point a year 2022–2025; 54% of registrants sign up 8+ days before; average attendees 239 (+11% YoY). ON24 counts on-demand viewers as attendees, which raises its attendance rate compared with live-only measures. [vendor: ON24 sells webinar software] [snippet-only; figures inconsistent across ON24 pages, so the playbook quotes ranges]
2. **Goldcast. *The 2025 B2B Webinar Benchmark Report*.** https://www.goldcast.io/reports/b2b-webinar-benchmark-report-2025 ; blog https://www.goldcast.io/blog-post/insights-from-the-2025-b2b-webinar-benchmark-report
   - 19,531 webinars across 418 B2B brands, calendar year 2024. Average attendance 33% of registrants; average 238 registrants; nearly 80% of webinars made available on demand; 45–60 minutes most common length; average watch time 29 minutes; Monday and Tuesday produced more attendees than the more common Wednesday/Thursday slots. [vendor: Goldcast sells event/webinar software] [snippet-only]
3. **Bizzabo. *2025 State of Events*.** https://www.bizzabo.com/blog/get-2025-events-benchmark-report ; PDF https://welcome.bizzabo.com/hubfs/Q125_BenchmarkingReport_2.18.pdf
   - November 2024 survey of 1,500+ event organizers and attendees. 78% of organizers say in-person events have unmatched impact on organisational goals; 24% named sales pipeline growth as the primary focus for conventions (16% in 2023). [vendor: Bizzabo sells event software] [snippet-only; self-reported opinion, not measured outcomes]
4. **Forrester. "The Global State Of B2B Events — Eight Key Findings From Forrester's 2024 B2B Event Trends Survey".** https://www.forrester.com/blogs/the-global-state-of-b2b-events-8-key-findings-from-the-forrester-2024-b2b-event-trends-survey/
   - 58% of marketers plan more small hosted/owned in-person events (fastest-growing type); 92% plan to improve post-event attendee follow-up; 77% want year-round attendee engagement. [vendor: analyst survey; Forrester also runs events] [snippet-only]
5. **Forrester. Buyers' Journey Survey 2025** (via "Three Realities About B2B Buying Networks" and related blogs). https://www.forrester.com/blogs/three-realities-about-b2b-buying-networks/

Younger buyers include external influencers such as online community members and industry conference attendees. [vendor: analyst survey] [snippet-only]

### YouTube (first-party)

6. **YouTube Blog (Goodrow, C.). "On YouTube's recommendation system".** 2021-09. https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/on-youtubes-recommendation-system/ ; also How YouTube Works https://www.youtube.com/howyoutubeworks/recommendations/ and YouTube Help 16533387, 16089387.
   - Signals: clicks, watch time, survey responses, sharing, likes, dislikes. "Valued watchtime" = watch time viewers rate 4 or 5 stars in 1–5 surveys; a model predicts survey answers for everyone. [first-party] [snippet-only; already in research/content-social-pr.md]
7. **YouTube Help. "How YouTube search works"** (answer 16090438) and How YouTube Works "Navigating YouTube Search". https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16090438
   - Three main elements: relevance (title, tags, description, video content matching the query), engagement (e.g. watch time of a video for a particular query), quality (signals of expertise, authoritativeness and trustworthiness on a topic); plus personalisation from search and watch history. YouTube does not accept payment for organic search placement. [first-party] [snippet-only]
8. **YouTube Help. "Test & compare thumbnails" / "A/B test titles & thumbnails"** (answers 13861714, 16391400); YouTube Blog "Rene's Top Five on YouTube: June 14, 2024". https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13861714 ; https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400 ; https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/renes-top-five-june-14-2024/
   - Up to 3 thumbnails (newer help page: titles and thumbnails, or combinations) tested at the same time on roughly equal audience segments; each viewer sees one variant; results judged on **watch time share**, not click-through rate, because watch time includes both clicks and retention; tests take a few days to 2 weeks; a "no winner" result means variants performed similarly. Rolled out in 2024 to channels with Advanced Features; eligible on public long-form videos and podcast episodes. [first-party] [snippet-only; exact date when title testing was added not confirmed]
9. **YouTube Help. "Measure key moments for audience retention"** (answer 9314415). https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415
   - Four moment types: Intro (share of viewers still watching after the first 30 seconds), Top moments, Spikes (rewatched or shared), Dips (skipped or abandoned). [first-party] [snippet-only]
10. **YouTube Help. "Video Chapters"** (answer 9884579). https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579
   - Chapters from timestamps in the description: first must be 00:00, at least three in ascending order, each at least 10 seconds. [first-party] [snippet-only]
11. **YouTube Help. "Understand three-minute YouTube Shorts"** (answer 15424877); YouTube Blog "What are 'Engaged' Views on YouTube?"; YouTube Help on Shorts and recommendations. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877 ; https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/engaged-views-youtube-explained/
   - Square or vertical videos up to 3 minutes uploaded on or after 2024-10-15 are Shorts. From 2025-03-31 Shorts views count every start or replay; the older metric is now "Engaged views". Snippet also reported that from 2026-08-24 views across all formats count from the first frame. YouTube says Shorts performance does not hurt long-form recommendations. [first-party] [snippet-only; the 2026 view-definition change and the Shorts/long-form statement were not tied to a single page URL]

### Video ads and video in Google Search

12. **Google Ads Help. "Skippable in-stream ads"** (answer 6055025). https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6055025
   - Skip option after 5 seconds. With CPV bidding you pay when a viewer watches 30 seconds (or the whole ad if shorter) or interacts, whichever comes first; with CPM, target CPA or maximize conversions you pay by impression. [first-party] [snippet-only]
13. **Google Ads Help. Demand Gen** (answers 13695777, 15110871, 15973205). https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/15110871
   - Video action campaigns are being upgraded to Demand Gen. Placements: in-feed (YouTube Home, Watch Next, Search; Discover; Gmail), skippable in-stream, Shorts, plus Google Display Network; lookalike segments from first-party seed lists; video and image in one campaign; channel controls. [first-party] [snippet-only]
14. **Google Search Central. "Video SEO best practices"; Video structured data (VideoObject, Clip, SeekToAction).** https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/video ; https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/video
   - For video features in Search (video results, Video mode, key moments, previews), create a dedicated watch page where the video is the main content; keep video files at stable, fetchable URLs. Key moments via Clip (exact segments and labels) or SeekToAction (Google finds moments itself). [first-party] [snippet-only]
15. **Chrome for Developers. "Autoplay policy in Chrome".** https://developer.chrome.com/blog/autoplay
   - Muted autoplay is always allowed; autoplay with sound only after user interaction, high Media Engagement Index, or installed site. [first-party] [snippet-only]

### Video effect on landing pages (weak evidence)

16. **Wyzowl. "Video Marketing Statistics 2026" (State of Video Marketing survey, 12th year) and "The Explainer Video Effect".** https://wyzowl.com/video-marketing-statistics/ ; https://wyzowl.com/explainer-video-conversion-rate/
   - Survey figures: 85% of people say a video convinced them to buy; 96% have watched an explainer video; 93% of marketers say video gives good ROI. Claim that an explainer video on a landing page can raise conversions "by up to 80%". [vendor: Wyzowl sells explainer videos; consumer and marketer self-reports] [snippet-only]
17. **"Up to 80%/86%" landing-page video lift.** A 2026 write-up (foundrycro.com) traces the widely repeated 80–86% figure to EyeView Digital, a video-ad vendor, from pre-2020 data that has not been published as a method. The same write-up cites a Vmaker test where click-to-play beat autoplay, and a Groove case study (testimonial video in hero, conversion 2.3% to 4.3%). [vendor/secondary] [secondary; none re-verified; not used as evidence of size of effect]

## Open questions

- ON24 webinar figures differ between ON24 pages (57% vs 60% registrant-to-attendee; 43% vs 50%+ on-demand). The full PDF was not accessible. Treat as "roughly half to 60%, with on-demand counted"; Goldcast's 33% is closer to a live-show rate.
- No independent (non-vendor) study found on cost per meeting or cost per opportunity for trade shows vs dinners. The playbook's ranking of event types is practitioner opinion.
- No controlled, published study found on autoplay vs click-to-play or on video vs no video on B2B landing pages. Test it yourself (see experimentation).
- Exact launch date of title testing in Test & Compare (help page 16391400) not confirmed.
- YouTube statement that Shorts do not hurt long-form recommendations came from a help/community snippet; page not identified exactly.
- The 2026-08-24 "views count from the first frame" change appeared in one snippet; not confirmed on a YouTube Blog page.
