### YouTube (first-party)

6. **YouTube Blog (Goodrow, C.). "On YouTube's recommendation system".** 2021-09. https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/on-youtubes-recommendation-system/ ; also How YouTube Works https://www.youtube.com/howyoutubeworks/recommendations/ and YouTube Help 16533387, 16089387.
   - Signals: clicks, watch time, survey responses, sharing, likes, dislikes. "Valued watchtime" = watch time viewers rate 4 or 5 stars in 1–5 surveys; a model predicts survey answers for everyone. [first-party] [read 2026-10-05; already in research/content-social-pr.md]
7. **YouTube Help. "How YouTube search works"** (answer 16090438) and How YouTube Works "Navigating YouTube Search". https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16090438
   - Three main elements: relevance (title, tags, description, video content matching the query), engagement (e.g. watch time of a video for a particular query), quality (signals of expertise, authoritativeness and trustworthiness on a topic); plus personalisation from search and watch history. YouTube does not accept payment for organic search placement. [first-party] [read 2026-10-05]
8. **YouTube Help. "Test & compare thumbnails" / "A/B test titles & thumbnails"** (answers 13861714, 16391400); YouTube Blog "Rene's Top Five on YouTube: June 14, 2024". https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13861714 ; https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400 ; https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/renes-top-five-june-14-2024/
   - Up to 3 thumbnails (newer help page: titles and thumbnails, or combinations) tested at the same time on roughly equal audience segments; each viewer sees one variant; results judged on **watch time share**, not click-through rate, because watch time includes both clicks and retention; tests take a few days to 2 weeks; a "no winner" result means variants performed similarly. Rolled out in 2024 to channels with Advanced Features; eligible on public long-form videos and podcast episodes; not Shorts, scheduled Lives or Premieres (Live archives yes; Premieres after conversion); desktop YouTube Studio only; winner = highest watch time, or first-uploaded option if no clear winner. [first-party] [verified-search: support.google.com, 2026-10-04] Title A/B testing announced in YouTube Blog "New in YouTube Studio: Ask AI, Title A/B Testing & More" (Made on YouTube 2025), https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-studio-made-on-youtube-2025/ [first-party] [verified-search: blog.youtube, title only, 2026-10-04]
9. **YouTube Help. "Measure key moments for audience retention"** (answer 9314415). https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415
   - Four moment types: Intro (share of viewers still watching after the first 30 seconds), Top moments, Spikes (rewatched or shared), Dips (skipped or abandoned). [first-party] [read 2026-10-05]
10. **YouTube Help. "Video Chapters"** (answer 9884579). https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579
   - Chapters from timestamps in the description: first must be 00:00, at least three in ascending order, each at least 10 seconds. [first-party] [read 2026-10-05]
11. **YouTube Help. "Understand three-minute YouTube Shorts"** (answer 15424877); YouTube Blog "What are 'Engaged' Views on YouTube?"; YouTube Help on Shorts and recommendations. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877 ; https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/engaged-views-youtube-explained/
   - Square or vertical videos up to 3 minutes uploaded on or after 2024-10-15 are Shorts. From 2025-03-31 Shorts views count every start or replay, no minimum watch time; the older metric is now "Engaged views"; YPP eligibility and Shorts revenue still based on engaged views [verified-search: support.google.com (answer 9082582) + blog.youtube, 2026-10-04]. Snippet also reported that from 2026-08-24 views across all formats count from the first frame. YouTube says Shorts performance does not hurt long-form recommendations (separate recommendation systems per format): YouTube Blog "Shorts truths: Debunking common myths about YouTube Shorts", https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/debunking-common-myths-about-youtube-shorts/ [first-party] [verified-search: blog.youtube, 2026-10-04]. YouTube Help ("How engagement metrics are counted", answer 2991785; "Understand your YouTube content performance", answer 12220281) states: from 2026-08-24 views are counted the moment a video starts to play across all formats (Shorts, long-form, live), with no change to YPP earnings or eligibility. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2991785 [first-party] [read 2026-10-05]

### Video ads and video in Google Search

12. **Google Ads Help. "Skippable in-stream ads"** (answer 6055025). https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6055025
   - Skip option after 5 seconds. With CPV bidding you pay when a viewer watches 30 seconds (or the whole ad if shorter) or interacts, whichever comes first; with CPM, target CPA or maximize conversions you pay by impression. [first-party] [read 2026-10-05]
13. **Google Ads Help. Demand Gen** (answers 13695777, 15110871, 15973205). https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/15110871
   - Video action campaigns have been upgraded to Demand Gen: new ones could not be created from April 2025, and the last were upgraded automatically by April 2026 (answer 15110871). Placements: in-feed (YouTube Home, Watch Next, Search; Discover; Gmail; Maps), skippable in-stream, Shorts, plus Google Display Network (Display campaigns are also moving into Demand Gen from June 2026); lookalike segments from first-party seed lists; video and image in one campaign; channel controls. [first-party] [read 2026-10-05; corrected: the VAC upgrade is complete, not in progress]
14. **Google Search Central. "Video SEO best practices"; Video structured data (VideoObject, Clip, SeekToAction).** https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/video ; https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/video
   - For video features in Search (video results, Video mode, key moments, previews), create a dedicated watch page where the video is the main content; keep video files at stable, fetchable URLs. Key moments via Clip (exact segments and labels) or SeekToAction (Google finds moments itself). [first-party] [read 2026-10-05]
15. **Chrome for Developers. "Autoplay policy in Chrome".** https://developer.chrome.com/blog/autoplay
   - Muted autoplay is always allowed; autoplay with sound only after user interaction, high Media Engagement Index, or installed site. [first-party] [read 2026-10-05]

### Video effect on landing pages (weak evidence)

16. **Wyzowl. "Video Marketing Statistics 2026" (State of Video Marketing survey, 12th year) and "The Explainer Video Effect".** https://wyzowl.com/video-marketing-statistics/ ; https://wyzowl.com/explainer-video-conversion-rate/
   - 2026 survey (266 respondents, late 2025, marketers and consumers): 85% of people say a video convinced them to buy; 96% have watched an explainer video; 82% of marketers say video gives good ROI (93% was the previous year's figure). The explainer page says adding an explainer video to a landing page "can boost conversions by up to 80%", with no source given. [vendor: Wyzowl sells explainer videos; consumer and marketer self-reports] [read 2026-10-05 via web.archive.org snapshots of 2026-10-03 and 2026-09-26; corrected: ROI figure is 82% in the 2026 edition]
17. **"Up to 80%/86%" landing-page video lift.** A 2026 write-up (foundrycro.com) traces the widely repeated 80–86% figure to EyeView Digital, a video-ad vendor, from pre-2020 data that has not been published as a method. The same write-up cites a Vmaker test where click-to-play beat autoplay, and a Groove case study (testimonial video in hero, conversion 2.3% to 4.3%). [vendor/secondary] [secondary; none re-verified; not used as evidence of size of effect]

## Open questions

- ON24 webinar figures differ between ON24 reports (57% in the 2025 report vs 60% in the 2026 report; 43–45% on demand in the body text vs a headline 50%). The full PDF was not accessible. Treat as "roughly half to 60%, with on-demand counted"; Goldcast's 33% is closer to a live-show rate.
- No independent (non-vendor) study found on cost per meeting or cost per opportunity for trade shows vs dinners. The playbook's ranking of event types is practitioner opinion.
- No controlled, published study found on autoplay vs click-to-play or on video vs no video on B2B landing pages. Test it yourself (see experimentation).
- Exact launch date of title testing in Test & Compare: announced at Made on YouTube 2025 (blog.youtube); exact rollout date not confirmed.
- Resolved 2026-10-04: Shorts/long-form statement is from the YouTube Blog "Shorts truths" post (item 11).
- Resolved 2026-10-05: the 2026-08-24 "views count from the moment a video starts to play" change is on YouTube Help (answers 2991785 and 12220281).
