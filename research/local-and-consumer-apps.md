## §1 Local services

1. **Federal Trade Commission, "Federal Trade Commission Announces Final Rule Banning Fake Reviews and Testimonials"**, press release, Aug 2024. https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials — Bans creating, buying or spreading fake reviews (including AI-generated), incentives conditioned on review sentiment, undisclosed insider reviews; civil penalties for knowing violations. [first-party][verified-search: ftc.gov, 2026-10-04]

[first-party][verified-search: ftc.gov, 2026-10-04] No 2026 inflation adjustment (OMB M-26-11), so $53,088 still applies [verified-search: federalregister.gov, 2026-10-04].

## Open questions

- Will Apple be allowed to charge any commission on US linked-out purchases? Depends on the Ninth Circuit remand and Supreme Court case 25-1311; recheck after the decision (expected by mid-2027).
- Resolved 2026-10-04: Google prohibits asking questions before/while showing the Play review card (item 16a); Apple disallows custom review prompts (item 16). Whether Apple App Review rejects a pre-screen followed by the system prompt was not found stated explicitly.
- First FTC enforcement action under the review rule: an FTC complaint filed 11 May 2026 (N.D. Ill., 1:26-cv-05415, "premium home service") appeared in results but was not read; unknown whether it charges Review Rule violations.
- Adjust retention figures: publication year and sample period not confirmed; look for the latest Adjust Mobile App Trends report.
- Effect of the LSA move into Performance Max on lead cost and lead quality: no data yet (migration started Aug 2026).
- No independent (non-vendor) study found on hard paywall vs freemium for apps; RevenueCat data is observational and confounded by category.
- No source found for a utilisation threshold at which local businesses should raise prices instead of marketing more.
