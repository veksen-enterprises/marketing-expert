# App discovery outside the stores: video, web, communities, press, word of mouth — Research Notes

**Scope.** How small and indie mobile apps get their first users, and stay noticed, through channels other than App Store / Google Play search and editorial featuring: short-form video and creators, the web (landing pages, Smart App Banners, deep links, Google results, AI assistants), communities and launch sites, press and reviewers, referral and word of mouth, and the economics of who gets downloads at all. Paid user acquisition is covered only briefly (see `research/landscape-2026.md` and `knowledge/consumer-apps.md`). Store search, ratings prompts, ASO basics, ATT and web-to-app purchases are already in `research/local-and-consumer-apps.md` and are not repeated here.

**Access caveat (2026-10-05).** Pages were fetched directly with a generic browser User-Agent. `[read 2026-10-05]` = the page or PDF text was read; `; abstract` = only the abstract was read; `[snippet-only]` = only a search-engine summary was seen, so figures must be re-checked before quoting. reddithelp.com (403/Cloudflare), facebook.com business help (empty page), SSRN and the OpenAlex API (daily quota used up mid-session) could not be read. Founder case studies are **survivors**: we only hear from apps that worked, and most numbers are self-reported to press or on the founder's own blog.

Tags: [first-party] platform or regulator documentation · [research] peer-reviewed or preprint study · [vendor] data from a company with a commercial interest · [practitioner] founder or expert post, not a controlled test · [press] journalism, often quoting a founder.

### G. Referral, sharing, word of mouth, cross-promotion

## What this means for a small app team

1. **Expect the base rate to be near zero, and plan for spikes.** Downloads concentrate in very few apps: on Google Play in 2013 the top 1% of apps had about 80% of downloads [1, research]; the market behaves as "superstar", not long-tail [2, research]; among subscription apps that bothered to integrate a paywall SDK, only about 17% reach $1K a month and 5% reach $10K within two years [6, vendor]. Total downloads have fallen five years in a row while launches multiply [6, 7, vendor]. Every case below is a survivor.
2. **Most documented indie take-offs came from someone else's video, timed to a platform moment, and then from the chart itself.** Widgetsmith (iOS 14 widgets, an unpaid creator walkthrough) and Locket (widgets, founder TikTok then user copies with the same sound) both reached No. 1 without paid creators [10, 11, practitioner/press]. Chart rank then feeds more demand [3, 4, research]. You cannot schedule this; you can make the app easy to show on screen in 15 seconds, and be ready (onboarding, servers) when it happens — Locket's rating fell to 3.4 during its surge [10].
3. **Paid creator programmes at scale are the 2024–26 pattern for AI consumer apps, but the public numbers are self-reported.** Cal AI, Umax and RizzGPT founders credit creator/UGC video at volume and report multi-million ARR [12–14, press/practitioner]; no independent figures exist. The best causal study of creator promotion (Twitch, games) finds a small, short-lived effect and that only about one in six games would profit from sponsored streams [16, research]. Test with a few creators and a tracked link before committing to retainers; the brand is legally responsible for creators' disclosure [19, first-party].
4. **A creator who owns or co-owns the app is a different channel from a paid post.** Focus Friend rose as Hank and John Green posted to their own audiences [15, press]; Shipaton winners credit founders' own build-in-public video [20, vendor]. If a founder can be the on-camera creator, that is the cheapest version of this channel [practitioner].
5. **Communities give a few hundred to a few thousand installs per hit, repeatably if the post fits the community.** Parsnip: ~750 launch-week installs mostly from Reddit, 4,500 in two days from HN No. 1, 5,500 from a stranger's Reddit post [40, practitioner]. Söka: ~300 downloads in two days from Mastodon and personal networks; nothing measurable from Product Hunt [39, practitioner]. Rules: HN wants something people can try without sign-up and now rejects "quickly-generated one-offs" [35, first-party]; Reddit sets no sitewide ratio but bans inauthentic participation, and each subreddit's moderators decide [36, first-party]; Product Hunt features editorially on four criteria and publishes no featured rate [37, first-party]. Asking friends to upvote breaks HN rules [35].
6. **Self-reported "first heard about it" data puts communities and friends close to store search.** In one August 2026 US survey: store search 20%, someone I know 18%, ads 16%, forums/communities 14%, Google 11%, social 10%, AI assistants 10% [8, vendor; self-reported, vendor sells AI-visibility tools]. Apple, which sells search ads, says 65% of downloads "start" in store search [9, first-party; no method] — the two measure different moments (first awareness vs. the last step before download), so both can be true.
7. **Build the web plumbing once; it is cheap and measurable.** Add a Smart App Banner on iOS Safari [22]; use Universal Links and Android App Links for shares and referrals [23, 24]; if you used Firebase Dynamic Links, they stopped working on 25 Aug 2025 [26]; tag links so traffic shows up as Web Referrer / campaign (App Store) or UTM / third-party referrer (Play) [30, 31]; give each creator or community its own custom product page (now up to 70, each with keywords and a deep link) [32]. All [first-party].
8. **Google web results for apps are a minor, poorly documented surface.** App Indexing is retired [25]; the mobile app pack has no Google documentation and no frequency data was found [28, vendor]; SoftwareApplication markup affects rich results for your own page, not the app pack [27, first-party]. A landing page that ranks for "[task] app" and links to the store is the practical play [practitioner].
9. **AI assistants now name apps, mostly by reading store listings.** In AppTweak's May 2026 sample, 47.5% of ChatGPT's app-recommendation citations were App Store or Google Play pages and 27% the open web [33, vendor; method not disclosed]. So a clear, task-specific store description and website do double duty. "Apps in ChatGPT" (Sept 2026 in-chat suggestions) are ChatGPT-native apps, not mobile apps [34, press]. No independent study of how assistants pick mobile apps was found.
10. **Design sharing into the product; passive broadcast beats asking for invites.** In a randomised trial, automatic activity notifications to friends lifted adoption 246% vs. 98% more for adding personal invites [42, research]. Respect platform limits: no forced reviews or forced installs of other apps, no "Select All" contact invites, no incentivised ratings on either store [45, 47, first-party]; iOS 18 Limited Contacts shrinks address-book invite flows [46, first-party].
11. **Press is a long shot for unknown apps; aim at standing indie slots and real news.** Twenty pitches produced zero coverage for one solo launch timed before WWDC [39, practitioner]; 9to5Mac runs a weekly Indie App Spotlight with an open contact [41]. Free app promo codes still exist for press; IAP promo codes ended 26 Mar 2026, so use offer codes for in-app access [32, first-party]. eWOM from third parties matters more for paid apps [43, research; snippet-only].
12. **On paid, a $100 Apple Ads credit and Basic's cost-per-install with a cap are the lowest-risk first test** [49, first-party]; Meta app campaigns on iOS report via SKAdNetwork [50, snippet-only]. See `knowledge/consumer-apps.md` for the rest.

## Folklore and weak claims

- **"Only 10% of Product Hunt launches get featured" / "upvotes from 30-day-old accounts count more" / "45–55 upvotes per hour".** Repeated across 2025–26 launch guides; Product Hunt's own Featuring Guidelines give no rate or weighting [37]. Treat as unverified.
- **"TikTok gives every video ~200 guaranteed views."** One practitioner's assertion [21]; no TikTok source. TikTok's stated ranking factors are in `research/content-social-pr.md` item 12.
- **"Reddit's 10% rule."** Not a sitewide rule; Reddit's Rules set no ratio and the Help page says only that "some communities" use it [36].
- **"Add schema markup to get into Google's app pack."** Vendor advice [28]; Google documents SoftwareApplication markup for rich results on your own page, not app packs [27].
- **"A press feature drives 1,000–10,000 installs a day" and "No. 1 needs ~200,000 downloads in 24 hours."** Seen only in vendor blog summaries with no data; no primary source found.
- **"AI-referred users convert 31% better" / "ChatGPT recommends apps to 800M users."** Seen in vendor search summaries without method; not verified. AppTweak's citation shares [33] are the only figures read, and they lack a prompt count.
- **Cal AI's "250 creators on retainer".** Widely repeated; traced only to a GetLatka page and secondary write-ups, not a founder-authored source [13]. Even the acquisition close date conflicts between summaries.
- **"Product Hunt is dead" / "Reddit gives 3–8% conversion".** Blog syntheses of founder threads with no sample; one documented solo case found PH useless [39], but no denominator exists.
- **"Viral = K-factor above 1."** Locket and Widgetsmith spread through creators and charts, not measured invite loops [10, 11]; the one randomised study measured peer adoption lift, not K-factor [42].

## Open questions

- No peer-reviewed study was found that measures the causal effect of TikTok/Reels/Shorts posts, or paid UGC, on **app** installs. Nearest: Twitch and games [16], Facebook apps in 2009 [42].
- No current (post-2020) independent census of the download distribution for either store; PlayDrone [1] is from 2013. RevenueCat [6] covers only apps already monetising.
- How often Google shows the mobile app pack, and what decides inclusion, is undocumented by Google.
- Whether AppTweak's ChatGPT citation shares [33] hold across other assistants (Gemini, Perplexity, Siri with ChatGPT) and with a disclosed prompt set.
- Meta app-install campaigns for tiny budgets after ATT: Meta's help pages could not be read; check current SKAN/AdAttributionKit support and minimum learning-phase volumes.
- Reddit Help "Spam" and subreddit-level rules could not be read directly this session (403); re-check wording.
- Product Hunt's real featured rate and whether mobile-only apps are featured at the same rate as web products.
- Outcomes for the survivors here (Parsnip, Söka, Shipaton winners) a year later were not checked.
