## Open questions

- No controlled or peer-reviewed comparison of MQL-based vs opportunity/buying-group-based funnels; Forrester's evidence is client reports.
- Gartner's "time with suppliers" figure: 17% (2019) vs 24% (current page). Unclear whether the method changed or the number did.
- The day-one-list findings (Bain/Google, 6sense) are survey recall by buyers after the purchase; recall bias toward the chosen vendor is likely and not addressed in the snippets.
- Rep-free preference rose from 43% (2020 survey) to 61% (2024 survey) to 67% (2025 survey), but question wording may differ across surveys.
- Activation and PQL conversion benchmarks mix very different definitions; no dataset with a common definition was found.
- Bridge Group quota attainment (48%) is "share of AEs at quota at the median company", not the share of total quota achieved; capacity models need the latter.
- Involuntary-churn share (20–40%) comes from billing vendors' own customers; independent estimates not found.
- No 2025–2026 independent PLG benchmark replaced OpenView's; current "2025 OpenView" numbers on the web look fabricated or misattributed.
