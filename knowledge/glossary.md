Short definitions in plain English. "See" points to the playbook where the idea is used in depth. Formulas follow the definitions used in this knowledge base; companies often define them slightly differently, so always check the definition behind any number you compare.

## A

- **A/B test**: show two versions (A and B) to randomly split groups of users and compare a pre-chosen metric. See experimentation.
- **ABM (account-based marketing)**: sales and marketing work together on a named list of target companies instead of chasing individual leads. See outbound-and-abm, b2b-saas-sales-led.
- **ACV (annual contract value)**: the average yearly value of one customer contract. A $36,000 three-year deal has an ACV of $12,000. In retail, ACV means all-commodity volume, a store-size-weighted measure of distribution (see retail-cpg). See b2b-saas-sales-led, channel-strategy.
- **Activation**: the moment a new user first gets real value from the product (for example, a team sends its first invoice). Activation rate = share of sign-ups who reach it. In brand advertising, "activation" means short-term sales-focused ads instead. See self-serve-saas, brand-and-demand.
- **AARRR**: a funnel model: acquisition, activation, retention, referral, revenue ("pirate metrics", Dave McClure). See metrics-and-measurement.
- **AE (account executive)**: the salesperson who runs deals and closes them. See b2b-saas-sales-led.
- **Aha moment**: the action after which users understand the product's value; used to design onboarding. See self-serve-saas.
- **AI Overviews / AI Mode**: Google's AI-written answers shown in search results, which can reduce clicks to websites. See seo-and-ai-search.
- **AOV (average order value)**: revenue ÷ number of orders. See ecommerce-dtc.
- **ARPU (average revenue per user)**: revenue ÷ users (or accounts) in a period. See channel-strategy.
- **ARR (annual recurring revenue)**: yearly value of active subscriptions; MRR × 12. Excludes one-off fees. See b2b-saas-sales-led.
- **ASO (app store optimisation)**: improving an app's store page (title, keywords, screenshots, ratings) to rank and convert better in the App Store and Google Play. See consumer-apps.
- **ATT (App Tracking Transparency)**: Apple's 2021 rule that apps must ask permission before tracking users across other apps; it cut the data available for mobile ad targeting and measurement. See consumer-apps, paid-acquisition.
- **Attribution**: assigning credit for a conversion to the touchpoints before it (last click, data-driven). It shows correlation, not cause. See metrics-and-measurement.

## B

- **B2B / B2C / DTC**: selling to businesses / to consumers / direct to consumers through your own store rather than retailers. See ecommerce-dtc.
- **Beachhead**: a small, specific first market you can win quickly before expanding. See launches-and-gtm, competing-with-incumbents.
- **Blended (CAC, ROAS)**: averaged across all channels, including free ones. Blended CAC can hide a paid CAC that is much higher. See metrics-and-measurement.
- **Bottom-up sizing**: estimating a market by counting real buyers × what each would pay, instead of taking a share of a big industry total ("top-down"). See market-sizing-and-timing.
- **Brand building**: broad, memorable advertising that makes more people think of you when they need the category; pays back over months to years. See brand-and-demand.
- **Bullseye**: a method for choosing channels: brainstorm all, test a few cheaply, focus on the one that works (Weinberg & Mares, *Traction*). See channel-strategy.
- **Buying group**: the several people at a company who together decide a purchase. See b2b-saas-sales-led.

## C

- **CAC (customer acquisition cost)**: sales and marketing spend ÷ new customers in the period. "Fully loaded" includes salaries and tools, not only ad spend. See metrics-and-measurement.
- **CAC payback**: months of gross profit from a customer needed to earn back their CAC. CAC $1,200 ÷ $100 gross profit per month = 12 months. See metrics-and-measurement.
- **Canonical tag**: a line in a page's code telling search engines which URL is the main version when several URLs show the same content. See seo-content-and-architecture, international-seo.
- **Category entry point**: a need or situation that makes someone start looking for a category ("my laptop just died"). Brands try to be linked to these in memory. See brand-and-demand.
- **ccTLD**: a country domain ending such as .de or .fr. See international-seo.
- **Champion**: the person inside a buying company who wants your product and argues for it internally. See b2b-saas-sales-led.
- **Churn**: customers (logo churn) or revenue (revenue churn) lost in a period. Annual churn is not monthly churn × 12. See metrics-and-measurement, retention-and-expansion.
- **Citation (local SEO)**: a listing of your business name, address and phone on another site, such as a directory. See local-seo.
- **CM / CM2 (contribution margin)**: revenue minus the variable costs of an order (product, shipping, payment fees); CM2 also subtracts marketing. See ecommerce-dtc.
- **Cohort**: a group of customers who started in the same period, tracked over time to see retention honestly. See metrics-and-measurement.
- **Cold start**: the problem of a new marketplace or network with too few users on each side to be useful. See marketplaces.
- **Confidence interval (CI)**: the range of effect sizes that are consistent with the test data. A wide interval means the test cannot tell a small gain from a loss. See experimentation, metrics-and-measurement.
- **Consent Mode**: Google's way of passing each visitor's cookie-consent choice to Google tags; version 2 is required for ads measurement and remarketing in the EEA since March 2024. See paid-acquisition, privacy-and-marketing-law.
- **Contribution margin**: see CM.
- **Conversion rate (CVR)**: share of visitors, clicks or users who complete a target action. See landing-pages-and-cro, paid-acquisition.
- **Conversions API (CAPI)**: Meta's way to send conversion events from your server, not only through the browser pixel, to recover data lost to browsers, ATT and consent. See paid-acquisition, privacy-and-marketing-law.
- **Core Web Vitals**: Google's page experience measures for loading (LCP), responsiveness (INP) and layout stability (CLS). See landing-pages-and-cro, seo-and-ai-search.
- **Counter-positioning**: a newcomer uses a business model the market leader will not copy because copying would damage the leader's existing business. See competing-with-incumbents.
- **CPA / CPC / CPM**: cost per acquisition (per conversion), cost per click, cost per thousand impressions. See paid-acquisition.
- **CRO (conversion rate optimisation)**: systematically improving pages and flows to raise conversion rate. See landing-pages-and-cro.
- **CTA (call to action)**: the button or link that asks the reader to take the next step ("Get my quote"). See landing-pages-and-cro, messaging-and-copy.
- **CTR (click-through rate)**: clicks ÷ impressions. See paid-acquisition, seo-and-ai-search.
- **CUPED**: a statistical method that uses each user's behaviour before a test to reduce noise, so tests need fewer users. See experimentation.

## D

- **D1 / D7 / D30 retention**: share of new users still active 1, 7 or 30 days after install or sign-up. See consumer-apps.
- **Dark patterns**: interface designs that trick or pressure people (hidden cancel buttons, fake countdown timers, pre-ticked boxes); regulators treat many as illegal. See behavioral-science, privacy-and-marketing-law.
- **Dark social**: sharing that tracking cannot see, such as private messages, Slack groups and podcasts. See metrics-and-measurement, channel-strategy.
- **Data-driven attribution (DDA)**: Google's default model that splits credit across touchpoints using path data; still attribution, not a lift test. See metrics-and-measurement.
- **Deliverability**: whether your emails reach the inbox rather than spam. Depends on authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), complaints and list quality. See email-and-lifecycle.
- **Demand capture vs demand creation**: capture = winning people already looking to buy (search ads, review sites); creation = making more people want the category or you later (brand, content). See brand-and-demand.
- **Design partner**: an early customer who helps shape the product in return for early access, and often becomes a case study. See launches-and-gtm, b2b-saas-sales-led.
- **Distinctive brand assets**: colours, logos, characters, sounds and phrases people link only to your brand. See brand-and-demand.
- **Doorway page**: a page made mainly to catch a search (often a template with only the town or keyword changed) that adds nothing for the reader; against Google's spam policies. See local-seo, international-seo.
- **Double jeopardy**: the finding that small brands have fewer buyers, who are also slightly less loyal. See brand-and-demand.
- **Dunning**: recovering failed subscription payments (retries, card updates, reminder emails, grace period) to prevent involuntary churn. See self-serve-saas, retention-and-expansion.

## E–F

- **Earned media**: coverage and mentions you don't pay for, such as press articles and other people's posts. See pr-and-influencers, ai-assistant-visibility.
- **Embargo**: an agreement that a journalist will not publish news before a set time. See pr-and-influencers.
- **Employee advocacy**: employees sharing company news and content from their own social accounts, by choice. See organic-social-and-community, content-marketing.
- **ESOV (excess share of voice)**: your share of category advertising minus your share of market. Positive ESOV is linked with market share growth. See brand-and-demand.
- **Expansion revenue**: extra revenue from existing customers (more seats, higher plan). See retention-and-expansion, self-serve-saas.
- **Faceted navigation**: filters on listing pages (size, colour, price) that can create huge numbers of near-duplicate URLs for search engines. See seo-content-and-architecture.
- **FMCG / CPG**: fast-moving consumer goods / consumer packaged goods; everyday products people buy often (food, drinks, toiletries). See retail-cpg, brand-and-demand.
- **Forces of progress**: the push, pull, anxiety and habit that decide whether someone switches products. See customer-research.
- **Freemium vs free trial**: a free plan forever with limits, vs full access for a limited time. See pricing, self-serve-saas.

## G

- **GA4**: Google Analytics 4, Google's current web and app analytics product. See metrics-and-measurement.
- **Gated content**: content you can read only after filling in a form with your contact details. See b2b-saas-sales-led, content-marketing.
- **GBP (Google Business Profile)**: the free business listing that feeds Google Maps and the local pack. See local-seo.
- **GEO (generative engine optimisation)**: trying to be cited in AI answers (ChatGPT, AI Overviews). Much advice is speculation. Not the same as a "geo test". See seo-and-ai-search, ai-assistant-visibility.
- **Geo test (geo experiment)**: turning advertising on or off in some regions and comparing them with similar regions to measure lift. See metrics-and-measurement.
- **GMV (gross merchandise value)**: total value of transactions on a marketplace, before the marketplace's cut. See marketplaces.
- **GRR (gross revenue retention)**: share of starting revenue kept after losses and downgrades, ignoring expansion; never above 100%. See metrics-and-measurement.
- **Guardrail metric**: a metric a test must not harm (for example, refunds), even if the main metric improves. See experimentation.

## H–I

- **Holdout**: a random group deliberately not shown a campaign or message, used to measure the real effect. See metrics-and-measurement, email-and-lifecycle.
- **hreflang**: a tag telling search engines which language or country version of a page to show to which users. See international-seo.
- **ICP (ideal customer profile)**: a description of the companies or people who get the most value and are most profitable to serve. See b2b-saas-sales-led, customer-research.
- **Incrementality / lift**: the extra results that happened *because of* marketing, compared with what would have happened anyway. Measured by experiments. See metrics-and-measurement.
- **Information gain**: what a page adds that other pages on the same topic don't (original data, first-hand experience, a worked example). See seo-content-and-architecture, seo-and-ai-search.
- **Intent data**: signals (often from vendors) that a company is researching a topic, used to time outreach. See outbound-and-abm.
- **Internal linking**: links between pages on your own site; they help search engines find pages and understand which matter. See seo-content-and-architecture.
- **Involuntary churn**: customers lost because a payment failed (expired card, bank decline), not because they chose to leave. See self-serve-saas, retention-and-expansion.

## J–L

- **JTBD (jobs to be done)**: understanding purchases as "hiring" a product to make progress in a situation. See customer-research.
- **K-factor (viral coefficient)**: invites per user × share of invites that convert. Above 1 means each user brings in more than one more; rarely sustained. See referral-programs, consumer-apps.
- **Kill criteria**: conditions, written in advance, under which you will stop a project or test. See startup-risk-and-opportunity.
- **Lagging vs leading metric**: lagging = accurate but slow (revenue, NRR); leading = fast early signal that predicts it (activation rate). See metrics-and-measurement.
- **Lifecycle marketing**: messages (email, push, in-app) triggered by what a customer does or fails to do. See email-and-lifecycle.
- **Liquidity (marketplace)**: the chance a listing sells or a request is filled within a reasonable time. See marketplaces.
- **LLM (large language model)**: the type of AI model behind assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. See ai-in-marketing, ai-assistant-visibility.
- **Local pack**: the map with three business listings shown for local searches. See local-seo.
- **LSA (Local Services Ads)**: Google's pay-per-lead ads for service businesses, shown above the local pack. See local-seo, local-services.
- **LTV (lifetime value)**: gross profit expected from a customer over their life with you. LTV:CAC compares it with acquisition cost; 3:1 is a rule of thumb. See metrics-and-measurement.

## M

- **MDE (minimum detectable effect)**: the smallest change a test is designed to detect reliably. Halving it needs about four times the traffic. See experimentation.
- **Mental availability**: how likely buyers are to think of your brand in buying situations. See brand-and-demand.
- **MER (marketing efficiency ratio)**: total revenue ÷ total marketing spend, across all channels; also called blended ROAS. See ecommerce-dtc.
- **MMM (marketing mix modelling)**: a statistical model of sales over time that estimates each channel's contribution; needs years of data with spend variation. See metrics-and-measurement.
- **MPP (Mail Privacy Protection)**: Apple feature that preloads emails, making open rates unreliable. See email-and-lifecycle.
- **MQL (marketing-qualified lead)**: a person whose activity passed a lead-score threshold. Widely criticised because companies buy as groups. See b2b-saas-sales-led.
- **MRR (monthly recurring revenue)**: monthly value of active subscriptions. See metrics-and-measurement.
- **Multi-homing**: customers or suppliers using several competing platforms at once (a driver on two ride apps), which weakens network effects. See marketplaces, competitive-analysis.

## N–O

- **NAP**: name, address, phone; should be identical everywhere a local business is listed. See local-seo.
- **Network effect**: a product becomes more valuable as more people use it. See marketplaces, competitive-analysis.
- **noindex**: an instruction that tells search engines not to show a page in results. See seo-content-and-architecture.
- **North-star metric**: one metric that captures the value customers get and leads to revenue. See metrics-and-measurement.
- **NPS (Net Promoter Score)**: % promoters minus % detractors on "how likely are you to recommend us" (0–10). See referral-programs.
- **NRR (net revenue retention)**: revenue kept from a group of existing customers after expansion, downgrades and churn; can exceed 100%. See metrics-and-measurement.
- **ODI (outcome-driven innovation)**: a survey method that ranks customer outcomes by importance and satisfaction to find unmet needs. See customer-research.

## P–Q

- **Payback**: see CAC payback.
- **Peeking**: checking a test early and stopping when it looks significant; this inflates false positives. See experimentation.
- **Penetration**: share of category buyers who buy your brand in a period. Growth mostly comes from raising it. See brand-and-demand.
- **Performance Max (PMax) / Advantage+**: Google's and Meta's automated campaign types, which choose audiences, placements and bids themselves. See paid-acquisition, ecommerce-dtc.
- **Pipeline**: the value of open sales opportunities. See b2b-saas-sales-led.
- **PLG (product-led growth)**: the product itself drives sign-up, conversion and expansion, with little sales help. See self-serve-saas, channel-strategy.
- **PMF (product-market fit)**: a product that a clear group of customers keeps using and would be very disappointed to lose. See metrics-and-measurement.
- **Positioning**: the context you set so the right buyers see why you are the best choice compared with their alternatives. See positioning.
- **Power (statistical)**: the chance a test detects a real effect of a given size; 80% is the usual default. See experimentation.
- **PQL (product-qualified lead)**: a user or account whose product usage shows readiness to buy. See self-serve-saas.
- **Pre-mortem**: imagining a project has failed and listing why, before starting. See startup-risk-and-opportunity.
- **Product-led sales**: salespeople work only the accounts whose product usage shows readiness to buy, offering help rather than a cold pitch. See self-serve-saas, retention-and-expansion.
- **Programmatic SEO**: many pages built from a template and a dataset ("plumbers in Lyon"). See seo-content-and-architecture, marketplaces.
- **Quota and ramp**: a quota is a salesperson's sales target for a period; ramp is the time before a new salesperson reaches full productivity. See b2b-saas-sales-led.

## R

- **RCT (randomised controlled trial)**: an experiment where people (or companies) are randomly split into groups that get different treatments, so differences in results can be credited to the treatment. See startup-risk-and-opportunity, metrics-and-measurement.
- **Retargeting**: ads shown to people who already visited your site or app. Attribution often over-credits it. See paid-acquisition.
- **Retention curve**: share of a cohort still active over time; a curve that flattens above zero is a key product-market fit signal. See metrics-and-measurement, consumer-apps.
- **ROAS (return on ad spend)**: revenue attributed to ads ÷ ad spend. Platform ROAS is not proof of incremental return. See paid-acquisition.
- **ROI (return on investment)**: (return − cost) ÷ cost.

## S

- **SAM / SOM**: see TAM.
- **Sales cycle**: time from a qualified sales opportunity being created to the deal closing. See b2b-saas-sales-led, events-and-webinars.
- **Scaled content abuse**: Google spam policy against mass-producing low-value pages to rank, whatever tool made them, including AI. See seo-and-ai-search, seo-content-and-architecture.
- **SDR (sales development representative)**: a salesperson who books meetings for AEs, often through outbound. See outbound-and-abm.
- **Self-reported attribution**: asking customers "How did you hear about us?" on forms or calls; catches word of mouth, podcasts and communities that click tracking misses. See metrics-and-measurement.
- **SEO (search engine optimisation)**: making pages easier to find, understand and rank in search engines. See seo-and-ai-search.
- **SERP (search engine results page)**: the page of results for a search. See seo-and-ai-search.
- **Share of search**: your brand's search volume ÷ total brand searches in your category; a cheap proxy for share of mind. See brand-and-demand.
- **Share of voice (SOV)**: your share of all advertising (or mentions) in your category. See brand-and-demand.
- **Sherlocking**: when a platform owner (Apple, Google) builds a feature that copies a popular third-party app. See platform-and-feature-risk.
- **SKU (stock-keeping unit)**: one specific product variant (size, colour, flavour) that you stock and sell. See retail-cpg, ecommerce-dtc.
- **SQL (sales-qualified lead)**: a lead that sales has accepted as worth pursuing. See b2b-saas-sales-led.
- **SRM (sample ratio mismatch)**: when the groups in a test differ in size from the planned split, showing a broken setup; do not trust the result. See experimentation.
- **Statistical significance (p-value)**: how surprising the result would be if there were no real difference. "Not significant" does not mean "no effect". See experimentation.
- **Structured data (schema)**: code added to a page, usually JSON-LD using schema.org terms, that describes the content to search engines. See seo-and-ai-search, local-seo.
- **Switching costs**: the money, effort or risk a customer faces in changing supplier. See competitive-analysis.

## T

- **Take rate**: marketplace revenue ÷ GMV; the share of each transaction the marketplace keeps. See marketplaces.
- **TAM / SAM / SOM**: total addressable market (all demand for the category), serviceable addressable market (the part you can serve today), serviceable obtainable market (what you can realistically win in a set time). See market-sizing-and-timing.
- **Time-to-value**: time from sign-up or purchase until the customer first gets the result they came for. See retention-and-expansion.
- **Topic cluster**: a main page on a subject linked to several detailed pages on sub-topics. See seo-content-and-architecture.

## U–W

- **UGC (user-generated content)**: content made by customers or creators rather than the brand, often reused in ads. See ecommerce-dtc, pr-and-influencers.
- **Unit economics**: revenue and costs per customer or per order (CAC, LTV, payback, contribution margin). See metrics-and-measurement.
- **Utilisation**: booked or billable hours ÷ available hours. See local-services, professional-services.
- **UTM parameters**: tags added to links so analytics can record which campaign a visit came from. See local-seo, launches-and-gtm.
- **Value metric**: what you charge by, chosen to grow as customers get more value (seats, contacts, usage). See pricing.
- **Van Westendorp**: a pricing survey asking at what price a product seems too cheap, cheap, expensive and too expensive. See pricing.
- **Win rate**: share of qualified sales opportunities that become customers. See b2b-saas-sales-led.
- **WTP (willingness to pay)**: the most a customer would pay. See pricing.

## Budget and team terms

- **Objective-and-task budgeting**: set the goal, list and cost the work needed to reach it, and add it up, instead of copying a % of revenue. See marketing-budget-and-team.
- **70/20/10**: a rule of thumb for splitting spend between proven work, extending what works and new experiments. See marketing-budget-and-team.
- **Product marketing**: the role that owns positioning, messaging, launches and sales materials. See marketing-budget-and-team, launches-and-gtm.
- **Marketing operations**: the role that runs tools, data and reporting. See marketing-budget-and-team.
- **S&M**: sales and marketing expense together, often given as a % of revenue or ARR. See marketing-budget-and-team.
